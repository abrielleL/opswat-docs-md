# License

## [License Information](/docs/md-ot-security/v.2.2.6/operating/license#license-information)

The **License Information** is accessible under **MetaDefender Industrial Firewall & IPS** **Configuration** → **Settings → License → License Information**. User cannot change this information:

- **Deployment ID**
- **Product ID**
- **Product Name**
- **Expiration Date**
- **Customer Name:**
- **Max Devices Supported**
- **Supported Protocols:** Protocols supported by the license for this appliance. The appliance can support all protocols or a subset.

## [License Update](/docs/md-ot-security/v.2.2.6/operating/license#license-update)

The **License Update** is accessible under **MetaDefender Industrial Firewall & IPS** **Configuration** → **Settings → License → License Update.**

User can update License in this page to keep the MetaDefender Industrial Firewall & IPS up to date.
