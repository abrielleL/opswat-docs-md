# Connection Sessions

## [Connection Sessions](/docs/md-ot-security/operating/connection-sessions#connection-sessions)

**Connection Sessions** lists **active and recent sessions** for the scope you have selected (for example a **sensor**). Use it to inspect how devices are communicating for a period the product defines. Labels and columns follow your installed version.

## [Before you start](/docs/md-ot-security/operating/connection-sessions#before-you-start)

| Step | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Open the **MetaDefender OT Security** web address your administrator gave you and sign in. |
| 2 | Open **Management** so you can use **Settings** (for example via **Go to Management**). |
| 3 | In the header, pick the **enterprise**, **site**, or **sensor** scope that matches what you want to document or troubleshoot. |

## [Opening Connection Sessions](/docs/md-ot-security/operating/connection-sessions#opening-connection-sessions)

| Step | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | In the sidebar, open **Settings**. |
| 2 | Open the tab that lists connection-related tools—often **Component** or the closest match in your build. |
| 3 | Click **Connection Sessions** (wording should match the UI). |

## [Main grid](/docs/md-ot-security/operating/connection-sessions#main-grid)

The screen is usually a **table** of sessions (source and destination, protocol, state, time, and other columns). Filter or search when those controls are available.

## [Notes](/docs/md-ot-security/operating/connection-sessions#notes)

- Session retention and which fields appear depend on **policy**, **version**, and **role**.

- For views that cover more than one sensor, switch the header **scope** to **Enterprise** or the right **site** when your environment offers that option.
