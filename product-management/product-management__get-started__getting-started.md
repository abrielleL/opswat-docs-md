# Getting Started

This section guides you how to get started using Central Management to explore a whole new user portal experience and leverage the platform to manage OPSWAT products remotely, improving productivity flows, providing better visibility between team members and more.

To get started with Central Management, you can follow the below steps:

- [Sign up an OPSWAT account for FREE](https://id.opswat.com/register?redirect=https://my.opswat.com&app=appOCM0001&SAMLRequest=jZJRb8IgEMe%2FSsM7haK1LbEap1lmopnTuoe9sRaVpIWuR9327cdsTdyLWQIPwP%2F%2Bd%2Fc7xtOvqvTOsgFldIoCnyJP6twUSh9TtM8ecYymkzGIqmQ1n7X2pLfyo5VgPReogXcvKWobzY0ABVyLSgK3Od%2FN1ivOfMrrxliTmxJ5MwDZWJdqbjS0lWx2sjmrXO63qxSdrK2BEyJq5VffvqnhU1g%2FN9XvDcldhCklKc1RaXJJSwAM6WTIW7ialBb20sfVShW3NpfQqajr1O3n%2BZpSGiBvuUjRbPtCD0kYDymOWJTg4WE0wnEyGOABC1kyDIY0igunBWjlUoMV2qaIUcZw4FaYMcaDEQ8SP4rCN%2BRt%2Bo4flO5I3sPz3omAP2XZBm%2BedxnyXq8TcQLU8%2BeX7M0t%2BPvG4kobTf7HtqfazQS7sZ1VIRtcSSsKYUXPekxuy5n0x7%2B%2FY%2FID) , if you don't have one. You can use this account to access all OPSWAT Cloud services and also evaluate OPSWAT Cloud products for free.
- [Sign into My OPSWAT](https://my.opswat.com). My OPSWAT will set up an account for your organization if you belong to an organization that has licenses with OPSWAT; otherwise, you can access My OPSWAT as a personal user.
- Click Central Management to navigate to Central Management and start enrolling your OPSWAT products' instances to leverage the platform.

  - If you are in an organization, you will land into that organization on Central Management
  - If you are a personal user, Central Management will create a personal account for you.
