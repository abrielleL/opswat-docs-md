# Overview

## [About This Guide](/docs/product-management/get-started/user-guide#about-this-guide)

Welcome to Central Management component's document. This component is a part of My OPSWAT. This guide is intended to provide the information you need to:

- Manage your OPSWAT Products.
- Manage your users' permissions.
- Learn about frequently asked questions and our library of knowledge base articles

## [About Central Management component](/docs/product-management/get-started/user-guide#about-central-management-component)

Central Management is a component of My OPSWAT that allows customers to manage multiple OPSWAT products remotely, improve data flows visibility, and provide better reports for each product and entire account. It also provides critical information like product instance health, license, and connection status, report history, license, and audit trails.

**Key features**

- Manage OPSWAT on-premises product instances.
- Centralize the engine updates for OPSWAT Products.
