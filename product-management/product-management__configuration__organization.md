# Organization

The **Organization** tab of the **Global** settings page contains settings related to the current organization that your user session belongs to.

If your user account belongs to multiple organizations, to change organization, open the drop-down menu next to your username in the top-right corner of the web interface and under **Switch Organization** click the Organization you want to switch to.
