# Products

The **Products** tab contains the settings related to managed OPSWAT products.

## [**Products** **Updates**](/docs/product-management/configuration/product-settings#products-updates)

The **Products** **Updates** section contains settings related to how the managed product instances are updated.

The **Auto**-**update when a new version is available** setting is enabled by default. When it's enabled, applicable product instances will auto-update when OPSWAT releases a new version of a product. Currently, auto-update feature is only available for MetaDefender Kiosk L-Series and MetaDefender Media Firewall.
