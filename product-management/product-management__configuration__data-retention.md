# Data Retention

The Data Retention setting control how the account's persistent data, includes event logs and product reports, is stored in My OPSWAT. The default length is 30 days and can be extended to a maximum of 365 days.

You can locate the data retention setting by navigating to **Settings > Global**, and selecting the **Organization** tab
