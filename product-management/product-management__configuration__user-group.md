# Users & Groups

The **User & Groups** tab displays a list of users of the organization that you are currently in.

**To update permissions for users,**

- Select users you would like to update
- Select Update Role from the Actions dropdown menu
- In the Update Role dialog, select the desired role then enter your user's PIN if required and click **Update** to confirm your choice.
