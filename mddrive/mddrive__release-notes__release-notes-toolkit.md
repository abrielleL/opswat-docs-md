**Improvements and Bug Fixes**

- **Differential scan days:** A device with large storage no longer shows an incorrect three-day limit when configuring differential scans, so the maximum configurable period reflects the actual capacity available on the Drive.
- **Smart Scan configuration:** Smart Scan now shows all licensed anti-malware engines immediately after enrollment, ensuring drive capabilities are visible from day one without requiring manual reconfiguration. **This will take affect after Drive is re-enrolled. Follow the instruction here** [Enroll MetaDefender Drive to Central Management Server from MetaDefender Drive Toolkit](/docs/mddrive/configuration/enroll-cm-from-toolkit)
