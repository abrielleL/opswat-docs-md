# Enroll MetaDefender Drive to Central Management Server from MetaDefender Drive with Smart Touch

From MetaDefender Drive with Smart Touch version 2.0.0 and later, MetaDefender Drive can be enrolled in Central Management directly from Smart Touch, without having to boot the Drive on a PC. Once connected, enrollment is reflected across Smart Touch, Drive, and DTK, and settings and reports begin syncing with cloud or on-premises Central Management.

Follow these steps to enroll from Smart Touch

1. From **Smart Touch**, tap **Settings**, then tap **My OPSWAT Central Management Enrollment**

2. Notice the warning, make sure it is not scanning or not in Secure File Storage mode, then tap **Setup**

If Smart Touch is being managed by Centralized Scanning Profile, you have to enter the OTP to authorize your self as a valid approver

3. Enter the URL and Registration Code of Central Management

4. Tap **Enroll**

5. Notice the **warning about interruption**

6. Tap Enroll button
7. Wait for it **Enrolling**...

6. If entered information is correct, Success message will appear, and the Drive has been enrolled successfully
