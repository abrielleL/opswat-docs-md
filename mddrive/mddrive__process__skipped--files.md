# Skipped Files in Results

[Manually Skipped Files](https://app.developerhub.io/metadefender-drive/v4.4.9/process/processing-your-device-with-metadefender-drive#manually-skip-files-during-scan)

Files can be skipped during a scan for multiple reasons.

Info

Skipped files result in a **UNSAFE DEVICE** final verdict, even when there are no detected files under **Potential Infection**, **Potential Vulnerabilities**, **Sensitive Data**, **Data Integrity** or **Country of Origin** lists.
