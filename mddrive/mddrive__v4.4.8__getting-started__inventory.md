# Inventory

The **Inventory** page shows a general overview of each drive's usage by displaying stats about the **number of scans,** **infected files found**, **vulnerabilities detected**, **potential data leaks** (files with sensitive data), and **Country of Origin** non-compliant files. These totals count both **Bare Meta** and **In-Session** scans.

You can view more details about the scan history performed on a specific drive by clicking the **View Reports** button next to this drive:

The list includes In-Session scan reports alongside pre-boot ones, newest first. An In-Session entry is labelled by its scan type, or **In-Session Scan** when no scan type is recorded, and shows the number of processed files and the issues detected. Opening it gives the same totals and device properties as a pre-boot report.
