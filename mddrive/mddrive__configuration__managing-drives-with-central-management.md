# Manage MetaDefender Drives with OPSWAT Management

### [Drive can be enrolled to SaaS or On-Prem Central Management Servers in 3 different ways](/docs/mddrive/configuration/managing-drives-with-central-management#drive-can-be-enrolled-to-saas-or-on-prem-central-management-servers-in-3-different-ways)

- [Enroll MetaDefender Drive to Central Management Server from MetaDefender Drive User Interface](/docs/mddrive/configuration/enroll-cm-from-drive)
- [Enroll MetaDefender Drive to Central Management Server from MetaDefender Drive Toolkit](/docs/mddrive/configuration/enroll-cm-from-toolkit)
- [Enroll MetaDefender Drive to Central Management Server from MetaDefender Drive with Smart Touch](/docs/mddrive/configuration/enroll-cm-from-smart-touch)

Enrolling with OPSWAT Central Management enables MetaDefender Drive administrators to centralize the following operations:

- [View, manage and adjust settings of all enrolled Drives with centralized policies](https://www.opswat.com/docs/cm/product-management/overview-policy-management)
- [Viewing and Managing scan reports per Drive](https://www.opswat.com/docs/cm/product-management/inventory)
- AutoSync scan reports after scans finish
- Exporting reports in PDF/TXT format
- [View specific details about the hosts involved in the scans](https://www.opswat.com/docs/cm/product-management/hardware-supply-chain#scanned-hosts-and-profiles)
- [Extract Scanned hosts information through Central Management's APIs](https://www.opswat.com/docs/cm/direct-instance-management#getdrivereports)
- [Send Email Notifications from My OPSWAT Central Management](/docs/mddrive/configuration/send-email-notifications-from-my-opswat-central-management)

For MetaDefender Drive to establish a connection with an OPSWAT Central Management server using **self-signed certificates**, please follow the instructions on the [Using Self-signed Certificates for OPSWAT Central Management](https://www.opswat.com/docs/mddrive/advanced-usages/ocm-https-certificates) page.

For more details on how to manage **MetaDefender Drive from OPSWAT Central Management**, please refer to the [Managing MetaDefender Drive](https://onlinehelp.opswat.com/centralmgmtv7/3.3.2.8._Managing_MetaDefender_Drive.html).
