# Changing Password

To change your password:

- Open the avatar menu in the upper-right corner and select **Profile**, or go to **Settings** → **Profile**.
- In the **Password** card, enter your **Current Password**, then the **New Password** and repeat it. The password must be at least 12 characters long, and the strength indicator shows how strong it is:

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/jdK6/knhqmrdtvs99d2jvlrfymy4mip6abzuc97swht9lyukof0arp7smkk7er6hpq84i.png)

- Click **Save**.

Changing the password requires a re-login: after saving, you are signed out and redirected to the login page, where you can sign in with the new password.
