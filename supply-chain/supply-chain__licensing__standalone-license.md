# Standalone License

The Standalone option is tailored for gateway deployments and offers maximum flexibility to meet specific security needs.

This packaging allows MetaDefender Software Supply Chain to function as a connector that integrates with your preferred software vendors.

Operating on a pay-as-you-need model, the Standalone license model enables you to configure specific functionalities, such as selecting the number of antivirus (AV) engines based on available MetaScan packages, SBOM, and Proactive DLP. By only paying for the services needed, you will have flexible control over resource allocation and the ability to scale independently of user counts.

**This Licensing Option is Ideal for:**

- Deployments requiring high customization or focused use cases such as managing third-party vendors or open-source software
- Small teams with specific security needs

**Included Technologies:**

- MetaScan Multiscanning: Choose from:
- Linux packages (5 engines, 10 engines, or MAX engines), or
- Windows packages (8 engines, 12 engines, 16 engines, 20 engines, or MAX engines)
- Learn more about AV engine packages [here](https://www.opswat.com/docs/mdcore/metascan-engines/metascan-engine-package)
- Proactive DLP: Full capabilities
- SBOM: Full capabilities
