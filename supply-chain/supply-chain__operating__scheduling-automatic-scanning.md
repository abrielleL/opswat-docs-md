# Scheduling Automatic Scan

Besides on-demand scans, MetaDefender Software Supply Chain can scan your repositories automatically on a recurring schedule. Scheduled jobs are listed in the **Upcoming Jobs** table on the Jobs page until they run.

## Schedule a scan

- Open the New Job panel, either with **Start Job** on a connection's Inventory page or with **Create Job** on the Jobs page.
- Select the repositories, references, workflow, priority, and an optional scan name, as for any other job.
- Switch to the **Schedule Scan** tab:

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/jdK6/zqvz2jbkljtoa0ndp73rrqrthth4mue1tpti3faa13fadwirg8t3elf6x0qis7te.png)

- Choose the **Reoccurrence** of the scan - Hourly, Daily, Weekly, or Monthly:

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/jdK6/64cc6m1cfq0db8rqfpji45fk35cys1scuel7s1wcoievnki4z13tziuy62gisxmj.png)

## Manage scheduled scans

Click **Create** to save the schedule. The job appears in the **Upcoming Jobs** table on the Jobs page, showing its frequency, the time remaining until the next run (**Starts In**), and the exact **Scheduled Date & Time**.

To remove a scheduled scan, click the delete button at the end of its row and confirm your choice in the Delete Job dialog.
