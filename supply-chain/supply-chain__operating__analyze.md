# Analyze

The Analyze page lets you scan individual files without connecting a repository - drop in a dependency manifest, an installer, an archive, or any other file, and MetaDefender Software Supply Chain analyzes it with the same technologies used for repository scans.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/jdK6/az937h7lakwm3j4upti02sk1e2n7gagelwceghzxv4qm2wb4drk10of8j404acka.png)

## Analyze files

- Drag and drop your files into the upload area, or click it to browse. Multiple files can be uploaded at once, up to the displayed size limit.
- Optionally give the analysis a **Job Name**, select the **Workflow** to apply, and add **Metadata** - any notes, tags, or identifiers you want associated with the uploaded files.
- Click **Analyze Files**:

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/jdK6/kk0kg12wh19wrbwykrqkr3z4h9fxuyds8duedxmuz5ys7vstyq6lhjmyua25r5ni.png)

## Analysis results

Each analysis appears in the **Analyze Jobs** table below the upload area, with its status and the detected threats, secrets, vulnerabilities, and license risks. Use the search field and filters to find a specific analysis, or **Delete all** to clean up the list.

Click a job to open the detailed results of the file. The Results cards summarize what each technology found - **Metascan** (antivirus engines), **Proactive DLP** (secrets), and **Software Bill of Materials** (vulnerabilities) - and the tabs let you drill into each area. The **Export as** menu downloads the results:
