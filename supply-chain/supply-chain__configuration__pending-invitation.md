# Pending Invitation

Accounts created through self-registration are not active right away - an administrator has to approve them first. Pending accounts are managed on the **Users** page.

- Go to **Users** in the left-side menu. Active accounts are listed in the **Active Users** table:

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/jdK6/qb47240uvei78cqcpxnlkqvjfu38p1qb7gxwwv5ekmc6p9mfaonpigykxf2of5c0.png)

- When there are accounts waiting for approval, a **Pending Users** table is displayed above the active users. Each pending account shows its name, username, email, and the permissions it will receive:

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/jdK6/yvhu9hw6yf2ebdki8yucuuc2frltagc1ewlobvd6sh4ebt7kvh5w4z2o87bp20ht.png)

- Use the actions at the end of the row to **Approve** (check mark) or **Reject** (cross) the account.

Approved users are moved to the Active Users table and can sign in immediately. Rejected accounts are removed. Until a decision is made, the user attempting to sign in sees the message **Account is not active**.
