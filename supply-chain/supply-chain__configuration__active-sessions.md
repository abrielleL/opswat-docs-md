# Active Sessions

You can review your **Active Sessions** in the **Profile** section on the **Settings **page.

- Open the avatar menu in the upper-right corner and select **Profile**, or go to **Settings** → **Profile**.
- Scroll to the bottom of the page:

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/jdK6/9ibiwtw37xkc7u178vygn6srlw52dkatq1gm3q14lmmdkkb9vqm3ejedpxuotx1c.png)

You can:

- Terminate an individual session:

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/jdK6/bydlxkob5n4r4hqatofwi9h9shepduzupmps2pxzrgfcfc6gkmubjarl63ayh8wx.png)

- Terminate every other session, except your current one:

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/jdK6/haxtu1e6dad0s4nj8ulbb15zpge1lt9mdmosemblgyprv1a5gagadau6ijtjuh1w.png)

- Terminate all sessions, including your current one:

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/jdK6/0ydw90urerdl9mnutbwjwvm5241vrgjadfzreriw9v2qh4d8p3wj2pjlbey7sw40.png)

For each action, you will be prompted to **confirm your password**:

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/jdK6/90fudzd1y2ksbzdj0fesscemglco7f5u4ifxfqupnwpm6tfmihlyxoj36g9fev0k.png)

Also, as an **administrator**, you can terminate other user's active sessions

- Go to **Users **page
- Select the **Terminate User Sessions **action for the designated user:

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/jdK6/v37gsm150vjq6u61hixxk02endopzztosnqpp8zwa8814prrlaate8g4ipy0t35j.png)
