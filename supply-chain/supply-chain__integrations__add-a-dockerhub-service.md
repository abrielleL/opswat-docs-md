# Add A DockerHub Service

In order to integrate DockerHub with MetaDefender Software Supply Chain, please follow these steps:

1. From the left side menu, navigate to **Inventory** and choose **DockerHub**.
2. Give your connection a name so you can easily identify it later.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/jdK6/zh6ylmi28nnlllxj0vqh3jyoqxnr7kilu4js2rce4n55j8or1ybzq1rckmutevid.png)

3. Enter your **DockerHub Username**. Scroll to the end of this page for more details.
4. Enter your **DockerHub Token**. Scroll to the end of this page for more details.
5. Select **Add Connection** in order to finish the process.

### Where can I find the DockerHub Username?

To get your username, please follow these steps:

1. Log in to [DockerHub](https://hub.docker.com/) and go to **My Account**, then click on **Security**.
2. Get your username from the top of the page.

### How can I generate the DockerHub Token?

1. Log in to [DockerHub](https://hub.docker.com/) and go to **My Account**, then click on **Security**.
2. Click **New Access Token**.
3. Give your token a description so you can easily identify it.
4. Set the **Access permissions** to **Read-only**.
5. Copy **Access Token** from the pop-up that is displayed.
