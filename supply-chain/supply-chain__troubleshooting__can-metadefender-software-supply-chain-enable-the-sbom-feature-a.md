# Can MetaDefender Software Supply Chain Enable the SBOM Feature as a Standalone Service Without Integrating with MetaDefender Core?

> **Check Your Version:**
>
> This article applies to all MetaDefender Software Supply Chain releases.

#### **Answer**

**No.** MetaDefender Software Supply Chain **must be integrated with MetaDefender Core** to enable and use the SBOM feature.

Please make sure that your MetaDefender Core subscription has the SBOM engine included.

You will need to add at least one Core instance to the MD Core Scan Pool before proceeding.
