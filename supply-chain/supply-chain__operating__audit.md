# Audit

The Audit page is the system events log of MetaDefender Software Supply Chain: it records what happened in the system and who did it, across scans, configuration changes, and user activity.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/jdK6/64dg0jb7e7r0c3w010xbnb40lk2ix04aonau7qk5rzpuhnh6iwe05sfijof3t4f1.png)

Use the tabs at the top to filter the log by area:

- **All:** every recorded event.
- **Scans:** scan-related events, such as started and completed scans or scanned files.
- **Configuration:** configuration changes, such as connection, notification, or workflow updates.
- **Users & Activity:** user management and sign-in activity.

Each event shows:

- **User:** who triggered the event - a user or the System itself.
- **Level:** the severity of the event, such as Info or Error.
- **Category** and **Type:** what area the event belongs to and what exactly happened.
- **Date & Time:** when it happened.
- **Details:** the full description of the event.

Use the search field and the per-column filters to narrow down the list, and the copy button at the end of a row to copy the event to your clipboard.
