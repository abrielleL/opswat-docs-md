# CVEs

The CVEs tab on the report page provides a consolidated view of all identified vulnerabilities detected during scans. The tab automatically deduplicates entries, ensuring each unique vulnerability appears only once, making it easier to prioritize remediation efforts and assess your software supply chain risk without redundant information.
