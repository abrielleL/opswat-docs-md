# What are the default login credentials for MetaDefender Software Supply Chain after a fresh installation?

> **Check Your Version:**
>
> This article applies to **MetaDefender Software Supply Chain **deployments on **Linux**.

## **Overview**

After a fresh installation of MetaDefender Software Supply Chain (MD SSC) using our official guide: [Installing using the command line - MetaDefender Software Supply Chain](https://www.opswat.com/docs/supply-chain/installation/installing-using-the-command-line), you are welcomed with the login panel instead of setup instruction. This is the expected behavior.

## **Explaination**

> MetaDefender Software Supply Chain **does not** have a pre-configured Administrator account or default password.

The system is designed so that the **first registered user** automatically becomes the **Administrator**.

### **Steps to Register:**

1. Navigate to `<your-mdssc-ip>:80`.
2. On the login screen, click on **Create new**
3. Complete the registration.
4. Log in using these new credentials.
