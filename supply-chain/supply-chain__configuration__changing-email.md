# Changing email

Every user can change their own email address from their profile.

- Open the avatar menu in the upper-right corner and select **Profile**, or go to **Settings** → **Profile**.
- In the **Personal Information** card, update the **Email** field and click **Save**. You will be asked to confirm your current password before the change is applied:

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/jdK6/7dc7l4no0ayhoks7hcuoccj3c0y9wwkj4pywwi7fqezybfyrm1usj4vrwrqid49h.png)

Changing personal information requires a re-login: after saving, you are signed out and redirected to the login page.

## Reviewing a user's details

Administrators can review the details of any account - full name, username, email, and permissions - by clicking the edit action of a user on the **Users** page:
