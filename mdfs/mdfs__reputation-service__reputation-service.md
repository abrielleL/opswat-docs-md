# Reputation Service

**OPSWAT | MetaDefender for Salesforce** helps protect your Salesforce environment from malicious IP addresses, URLs, and domains embedded in your records. Reputation Service helps identify and block threats hidden in links and network addresses across your Salesforce data by leveraging OPSWAT's comprehensive threat intelligence and dynamic sandbox analysis. The service automatically scans addresses found in Accounts, Contacts, Leads, Cases, Opportunities, and other Salesforce objects, detecting phishing URLs, malicious domains, and suspicious IP addresses before they can be used to compromise your organization.

- **IP, URL, and Domain scanning**
- **Threat intelligence lookups** (reputation checks)
- **Dynamic sandbox analysis** for deeper IP/URL/Domain inspection
- **Multi-object support** (triggers on Accounts, Contacts, Leads, Cases, etc.)
- **Malicious address notifications** to alert administrators
