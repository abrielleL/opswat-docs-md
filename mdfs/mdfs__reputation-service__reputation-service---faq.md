# Reputation Service - FAQ

**What limits are being consumed for Reputation Service?**

Reputation Service scans consume `reputation_api` limits, and Reputation Sandbox scans consume `sandbox_api` limits.

**What are the supported objects for Reputation Service?**

Account, Asset, Case, Contact, Contract, Event, FeedComment, FeedItem, Lead, Note, Opportunity, Order, Task, User.
