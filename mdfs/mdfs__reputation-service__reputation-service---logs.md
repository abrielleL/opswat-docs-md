# Reputation Service - Logs

As a Salesforce administrator, you will have access to the OPSWAT - Reputation Logs as shown below.

The **OPSWAT - Reputation Logs** report will provide you with high level information of the addresses (URLs, IPs, and Domains) that have been scanned in your Salesforce environment. Such information includes:

- **Log ID**: A unique ID (RS{00000000} format) created to track each address scanned
- **Scanned Address**: The URL, IP, or Domain that was scanned
- **Reputation Address Status**: The information in this column will indicate whether there is "No Threat Detected" with a given address or if the address has been flagged as "Malicious" by our reputation scanning technology
- **Address Source Object**: Where the address was extracted from
- **Created By**: The user who initiated the scan
- **Created Date**: The date when the scan was initiated
