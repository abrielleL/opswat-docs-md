# Product Overview

Summarize Page

**MetaDefender Cloud Email Security ™** is an advanced solution that bolsters Microsoft 365’s native email defenses with industry-leading threat prevention. It is purpose-built to detect and neutralize malware, zero-day exploits, and embedded file-based threats before they reach the inbox.

This solution is powered by OPSWAT’s Metascan™ Multiscanning, adding more AV (antivirus) engines, MetaDefender Aether Sandbox for advanced dynamic threat analysis, and Deep CDR™ (Content Disarm & Reconstruction) against file-based threats. By combining signature-based detection, behavioral sandboxing, and proactive content sanitization, email security extends beyond traditional defenses to identify and neutralize both known and zero-day threats. With multi-layered, proactive protection and advanced technologies designed to combat evolving attack techniques, the solution deploys rapidly and integrates seamlessly without disrupting mail flow.

## [Benefits](/docs/mdces3/getting-started/product-overview#benefits)

**MetaDefender Cloud Email Security ™** bolsters Microsoft 365's security posture with advanced threat and zero-day malware prevention, providing robust, multi-layered security complementing Microsoft 365’s native protection. OPSWAT’s exclusive MetaScan™ and Deep CDR™ technologies detect and neutralize known, unknown, and zero-day threats, reducing the risk of malware, ransomware, and file-based exploits.

- **Eliminates hidden, file-based threats before they reach users:** Disarm malicious content within attachments and embedded objects in real-time, preventing weaponized files from reaching inboxes, even if they’re unknown to antivirus engines, for true proactive protection.
- **Simplifies deployment with zero disruption to mail flow:** Integrates seamlessly with Microsoft 365, without requiring MX record changes or architectural reconfiguration, ensuring rapid setup and uninterrupted email delivery.
- **Enhances compliance and audit readiness with detailed reporting:** Provides centralized visibility and granular logging of emails, attachments, threat events, and policy action, helping organizations meet cybersecurity, regulatory, and internal compliance requirements.
- **Reduces SOC workload with fewer false positives and remediations:** By combining high-accuracy detection and sanitization, the solution minimizes alert fatigue, accelerates investigation time, and allows SOC teams to focus on real threats rather than sifting through false alarms.
- **Backed by OPSWAT's legacy of successfully protecting critical infrastructure:** Built by a cybersecurity leader and trusted by over 1,800 global critical infrastructure organizations, **MetaDefender Cloud Email Security ™** brings proven, industrial-grade threat prevention to enterprise email environments.
