# Data Retention

Summarize Page

**MetaDefender** **Cloud Email Security**™ stores different types of data across multiple systems. Each data type has its own retention period, designed to balance operational needs, security analysis, and privacy considerations.

## [Overview of Retention Periods](/docs/mdces3/compliance/data-retention#overview-of-retention-periods)

| Data Store | What it contains | Default Retention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content | Raw email data and attachments (Quarantined, Sanitized original etc.) | 30 days |
| Metadata | Email records (sender, recipient, subject, status, comments, activity history etc.) | 90 days |
| Statistics | Aggregated statistics used for dashboards | 365 days |
