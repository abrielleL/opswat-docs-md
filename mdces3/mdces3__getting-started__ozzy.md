# Chatbot (Ozzy)

Summarize Page

Ozzy is OPSWAT’s intelligent virtual assistant, designed to streamline your experience by providing instant support, accessing public documentation, and helping with customer success tasks. Integrated into the **MetaDefender Cloud Email Security ™** user interface, Ozzy offers quick and reliable assistance 24/7.

Info

Ozzy only has access to publicly available documentation and services. By chatting with Ozzy you agree to our privacy policy: <https://www.opswat.com/legal/privacy-policy>

## [Accessing Ozzy](/docs/mdces3/getting-started/ozzy#accessing-ozzy)

### [From the MetaDefender Cloud Email Security™ UI](/docs/mdces3/getting-started/ozzy#from-the-metadefender-cloud-email-security-ui)

1. On the **dashboard** or any main UI page, locate the **Ask Ozzy button**.
2. Click the icon to open the chat window.
3. You can now interact with Ozzy using natural language queries.

## [What Ozzy Can Help You With](/docs/mdces3/getting-started/ozzy#what-ozzy-can-help-you-with)

Ozzy is equipped to handle a wide variety of tasks and provide access to essential resources. Here's a breakdown of how Ozzy can support you:

### [Accessing Public Documentation](/docs/mdces3/getting-started/ozzy#accessing-public-documentation)

- Ask Ozzy for help with:

  - User guides and manuals
  - Deployment and configuration steps
  - Feature explanations and best practices
- Example: *“Ozzy, help me understand the dashboard.”*

### [Customer Success & Support Tasks](/docs/mdces3/getting-started/ozzy#customer-success--support-tasks)

- **Troubleshooting help**: Ozzy can guide you through basic steps to resolve issues.
- **FAQs**: Get quick answers to common questions about configuration, policies, or licensing.
- **Issue logging**: Ozzy can assist you in initiating a support ticket if the problem requires human assistance.
- Example: *“I’m having trouble with policy configuration.”*

### [Contacting OPSWAT Support Agents](/docs/mdces3/getting-started/ozzy#contacting-opswat-support-agents)

- If Ozzy can’t fully resolve your issue, he can:

  - Escalate the conversation to a live **Customer Success or Support Agent**
  - Help you **submit a ticket** with necessary details
  - Guide you through the **support workflow**
- Example: *“I need to speak with a support agent.”*

## [Tips for Effective Use](/docs/mdces3/getting-started/ozzy#tips-for-effective-use)

- **Be specific**: The more details you provide in your question, the better Ozzy can assist.
- **Use keywords**: If looking for documents, include keywords like “dashboard,” “policy,” or “integration.”

## [Need Further Assistance?](/docs/mdces3/getting-started/ozzy#need-further-assistance)

If Ozzy can’t fully resolve your issue or if you prefer human assistance, you can:

- Use Ozzy to **escalate the chat**
- Visit the [OPSWAT Support Portal](https://support.opswat.com/)
