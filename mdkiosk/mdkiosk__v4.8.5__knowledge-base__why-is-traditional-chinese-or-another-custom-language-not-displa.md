# Why Is Traditional Chinese or Another Custom Language Not Displayed After the Kiosk Upgrade?

**Check Your Version:**
This article applies to MetaDefender Kiosk upgrades from versions earlier than v4.7.0.

### [**Issue:**](/docs/mdkiosk/v4.8.5/knowledge-base/why-is-traditional-chinese-or-another-custom-language-not-displa#issue)

After upgrading MetaDefender Kiosk from a version earlier than v4.7.0 to any v4.7.x release, Traditional Chinese or other custom languages may not be displayed correctly.

Such as:

- **v4.6.0 → v4.7.6**
- **v4.5.6 → v4.7.7**

### [**Resolution:**](/docs/mdkiosk/v4.8.5/knowledge-base/why-is-traditional-chinese-or-another-custom-language-not-displa#resolution)

Please update using the recommended Upgrade Path:

- From **v4.6.0** → Upgrade directly to **v4.7.7**
- From **v4.5.x** → Upgrade to **v4.6.7** first, then to **v4.7.7**

Please refer to [Upgrading MetaDefender Kiosk - MetaDefender Kiosk Windows](https://www.opswat.com/docs/mdkiosk/installation/upgrading-metadefender-kiosk) for further instructions on how to upgrade Kiosk.

### [**Reference**](/docs/mdkiosk/v4.8.5/knowledge-base/why-is-traditional-chinese-or-another-custom-language-not-displa#reference)

For more details, please refer to the Kiosk 4.7.7 Release Notes: [Archived Release Notes - MetaDefender Kiosk Windows](https://www.opswat.com/docs/mdkiosk/release-notes/archived-release-notes#477)

- Bug fix: Resolved issue with missing translation text when upgrading to Kiosk 4.7.6

  - Kiosk resolved issues where some language translation texts could disappear when upgrading from older versions to Kiosk 4.7.6
