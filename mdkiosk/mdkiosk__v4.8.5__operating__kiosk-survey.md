# Kiosk Survey

As part of product improvement and development, OPSWAT recommends administrators of Kiosk to do a Net Promoter Score (NPS) survey. The survey is performed via an internet-connected Management Console and repeated yearly.

A popup with the survey link appears after an administrator successfully logs in. Once clicking on the link, the admin is navigated to a new tab for the survey. After completing the survey, the admin should click on **DONE** to prevent Kiosk to prompt for the survey again. The survey will be requested for again in another year.

The admin can ignore the survey link by clicking on **LATER**. Once an admin is ready to participate in the survey, clicking on the right bottom floater “JOIN KIOSK SURVEY“ will bring up the survey prompt again.
