**Downloading OMVA and OMVA Tool:**

The new version of OMVA and OMVA Tool are published on the MyOPSWAT Portal.

1. Go to the MyOPSWAT Portal website ([https://my.opswat.com](https://my.opswat.com/)) and log in to your account.
2. Navigate to the MetaDefender Kiosk page from the Home Page (<https://my.opswat.com/home/metadefender-kiosk>) and select the OPSWAT Media Validation Agent module.
3. You can then proceed to download OMVA and OMVA Tool.
