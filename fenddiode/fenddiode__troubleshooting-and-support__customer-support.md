# Customer Support

Need help? Please contact Fend support via any of the following channels:

- Create a ticket by logging into your MyOPSWAT account ([https://my.opswat.com](https://my.opswat.com/)):

  - Go to Support on the lefthand menu
  - Click on the "Need support, Start Here" bar, then click on "Click here to submit a case" text to begin making a ticket.

- Email: [fendsupport@opswat.com](mailto:fendsupport@opswat.com)
- Phone: 571-970-1382, select the support extension (Ext. 2)
