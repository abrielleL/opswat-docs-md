Welcome to the User’s Manual for the Fend Data Diode XE5, SE5, CE5, XE15, SE15, and CE15 Models.

## [**WARNING**](/docs/fenddiode/introduction#warning)

**Do not open the case of the diode or otherwise tamper with the unit. Tampering with the diode will permanently disable the unit.**

Opening the case of the diode is in violation of the terms and conditions of sale and will void the warranty. No returns or refunds will be accepted for diodes that have been opened or otherwise tampered with.
