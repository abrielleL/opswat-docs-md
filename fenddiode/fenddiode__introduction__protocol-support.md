## [Maximum Data Throughput Speeds by Protocol](/docs/fenddiode/introduction/protocol-support#maximum-data-throughput-speeds-by-protocol)

Maximum data throughput speed varies by series and protocol as outlined below:

| Protocol | 5 Series | 15 Series |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **UDP** | 5 Mbps | 15 Mbps |
| **TCP** | 5 Mbps | 15 Mbps |
| **TCP w/ TLS** | 4 Mbps | 4 Mbps |
| **FTP** | 5 Mbps | 15 Mbps |
| **FTPS** | 4 Mbps | 4 Mbps |
| **SFTP** | 2.8 Mbps | 2.8 Mbps |
