# Password Renewal

View as Markdown Summarize this page Ask ChatGPT about this page Ask Claude about this page Connect your AI tool

If this is your first login to MetaDefender® MFT or if prompted, you may need to reset your password. Enter your current password, then enter your new password twice, and click "Submit." Pay attention to the [password strength](/docs/mdmft/v3.11.5/end-user-guide/password-strength) indicator for security.
