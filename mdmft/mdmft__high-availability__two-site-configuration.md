### [Disabling the Reverse Proxy](/docs/mdmft/high-availability/two-site-configuration#disabling-the-reverse-proxy)

In the Two-Site topology, the MetaDefender MFT HA Controller™ does not handle client traffic and therefore does not need to operate as a reverse proxy. You can disable the reverse proxy with the configuration tool.
