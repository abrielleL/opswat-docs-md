# Submit OESIS OK Certification

View as Markdown Ask ChatGPT about this page Ask Claude about this page Connect your AI tool

Get your Anti-Malware or Disk Encryption product certified at the **Gold** or **Platinum** level, right from My OPSWAT Portal.

## [Get started](/docs/mdsdk/knowledge-base/submit-oesis-ok-certification#get-started)

1. Sign in to [My OPSWAT Portal](https://my.opswat.com/portal/oem-portal/oesis-submission). No account yet? Sign up first.
2. Go to **OEM Portal → OESIS Submission → Add Submission**.
3. Choose **OESIS OK Certification**.

## [Fill in the form](/docs/mdsdk/knowledge-base/submit-oesis-ok-certification#fill-in-the-form)

### [Installer](/docs/mdsdk/knowledge-base/submit-oesis-ok-certification#installer)

Provide the installer in either or both of these ways:

- **Application Package:** upload the installer. Maximum 5 GB.
- **Download URL:** a public http(s) link to the installer.

> **Note:** If you give both, we use the Download URL. If we can’t reach the link, we’ll ask you to fix it.

### [Application](/docs/mdsdk/knowledge-base/submit-oesis-ok-certification#application)

- **Application name:** your product’s name.
- **Version:** the exact version of the installer.
- **Operating system:** Windows, macOS or Linux. One OS per submission.

> **Tip:** When you add a package, we detect the name, version and OS and fill them in for you. Please check that they are correct.

### [Certification](/docs/mdsdk/knowledge-base/submit-oesis-ok-certification#certification)

- **Certification category:** Anti-Malware or Disk Encryption.
- **Certification level:** Gold (compatibility) or Platinum (compatibility plus effectiveness). See [What do Gold and Platinum mean?](https://www.opswat.com/partners/oesis-ok#program-tiers).

### [Test configuration file](/docs/mdsdk/knowledge-base/submit-oesis-ok-certification#test-configuration-file)

A .json file that tells OPSWAT how to install, activate and test your product.

- Download the template (.zip) from the form.
- Follow the README inside to fill in the file for your category and level.
- Upload the completed file.

> **Tip:** Not sure what to enter? Ask a technical person on your team. A complete, accurate file helps you get certified faster.

### [Optional details](/docs/mdsdk/knowledge-base/submit-oesis-ok-certification#optional-details)

- **License key:** allows OPSWAT to test all features. Enter it as text or upload a file (maximum 5 MB).
- **Additional information:** anything that helps testing, such as install steps, test accounts or known limitations.

### [Technical contacts](/docs/mdsdk/knowledge-base/submit-oesis-ok-certification#technical-contacts)

- **Contacts:** who OPSWAT contacts about technical issues.

## [Track your status](/docs/mdsdk/knowledge-base/submit-oesis-ok-certification#track-your-status)

No more emailing back and forth. See where your submission stands at any time.

- **All submissions in one place:** the **OESIS Submission** page shows your own submissions and your organization’s submissions.
- **We need more information:** you get an email with the reviewer’s note. Open the submission in My OPSWAT Portal, use **Edit & resubmit** to send the update within **30 days**. Otherwise, the submission is closed.
- **Your result is ready:** you get an email. Open the submission and click **Download Report** to get the detailed report and, if you passed, your badges.

**Possible results:** PASSED – Gold, PASSED – Platinum or FAILED. The awarded level can be lower than the level you requested, never higher.
