# OESIS OK FAQ

View as Markdown Ask ChatGPT about this page Ask Claude about this page Connect your AI tool 1. What does the OESIS OK certification actually test?

Detailed certification criteria can be found in our certification criteria, and in our [**method documentation**](https://software.opswat.com/OESIS_V4/html/c_method.html) for a more technical information.

2. Where can I ask further questions or for more details?

Contact [certification@opswat.com](mailto:certification@opswat.com) for any further details or to schedule a call with our team.

3. Are test results publicly announced, and what happens if my product fails?

No. All detailed OESIS OK test results are kept strictly confidential and are never publicly announced, including failed or incomplete evaluations. Your report is available only to you and your organization in My OPSWAT Portal. Successful certifications are publicly recognized unless you request otherwise.

4. How much does certification cost?

OESIS OK is a free program. Evaluation is completely complimentary for endpoint security vendors. There is no cost to apply or to be tested.

5. What's the difference between Gold and Platinum?

Gold certifies that your product is fully compatible with 100+ market-leading access control and encryption solutions. Platinum includes everything in Gold plus independent verification of your product's real-world security effectiveness.

6. Which products are eligible for certification?

Endpoint antimalware and disk encryption products are eligible. We will expand to more product categories in the future to make the program inclusive and offer broader coverage.

7. If my product failed, how do I get certified?

Your report lists the items to address. Fix them and create a new submission in My OPSWAT Portal. Need guidance? Contact [certification@opswat.com](mailto:certification@opswat.com).

8. What do I need to submit, and how long does it take?

Submit through the OESIS OK form in My OPSWAT Portal. You’ll need your installer (upload it or give a Download URL) and a test configuration file, built from the template on the form. You can also add a license key and any notes that help testing. We review new submissions within two business days, and you can track the status in the portal at any time. Most partners go from submission to badge within a few weeks.

9. What NFR license do I need to provide?

If your product needs a license to unlock all features, provide a one-year, not-for-resale (NFR) license for at least 10 seats in the License key field. You can enter it as text or upload a file (up to 5 MB).

10. When is it time to re-certify?

Re-certification is recommended at your next major version release, a rebranding, or any other major changes to your product.

11. How do I upgrade from Gold to Platinum?

Create a new OESIS OK submission in My OPSWAT Portal and choose the Platinum level. Questions? Contact [certification@opswat.com](mailto:certification@opswat.com).

12. How is my submission kept secure?

Files are uploaded securely through My OPSWAT Portal, directly to OPSWAT’s certification system, and are handled only by the OPSWAT team for certification. Your files and results are never shared with third parties. A Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (MNDA) is available on request.
