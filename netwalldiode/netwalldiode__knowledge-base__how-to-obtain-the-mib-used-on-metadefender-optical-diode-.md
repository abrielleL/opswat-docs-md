# How to obtain the MIB used on Metadefender Optical Diode?

This article applies to all Netwall models ( Optical Diode, Unidirectional Security Gateway, Bidirectional Security Gateway)

## [**Overview**](/docs/netwalldiode/knowledge-base/how-to-obtain-the-mib-used-on-metadefender-optical-diode-#overview)

Custom **Management Information Base (MIB)** files for **OPSWAT MetaDefender NetWall** are not publicly available and must be requested directly from OPSWAT Support. These MIBs are model‑ and version‑specific to ensure accurate SNMP monitoring and integration.

## [**Details**](/docs/netwalldiode/knowledge-base/how-to-obtain-the-mib-used-on-metadefender-optical-diode-#details)

- MetaDefender NetWall uses **custom MIBs** that vary by:

  - Product model
  - Software version
- OPSWAT Support validates the requested information before providing the appropriate MIB files.

## [**How to Obtain the MIB Files**](/docs/netwalldiode/knowledge-base/how-to-obtain-the-mib-used-on-metadefender-optical-diode-#how-to-obtain-the-mib-files)

1. Contact **OPSWAT Support** through the official support channel.
2. Provide the following details:

   - MetaDefender NetWall model
   - Installed software version
3. OPSWAT Support will review the request and supply the correct MIB files for your environment.

## [**Notes**](/docs/netwalldiode/knowledge-base/how-to-obtain-the-mib-used-on-metadefender-optical-diode-#notes)

- Using incorrect or mismatched MIB files may result in incomplete or inaccurate SNMP data.
- Always verify the product version before requesting or deploying MIBs.
