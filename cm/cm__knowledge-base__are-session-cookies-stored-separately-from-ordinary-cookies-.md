# Are session cookies stored separately from ordinary cookies?

> **Check Your Product:**
>
> This article applies to the current My OPSWAT Central Management Console viewed in Google Chrome, Internet Explorer, Microsoft Edge, or Safari browsers.

My OPSWAT Central Management uses local storage for most storage elements. The token provided in the storage is what allows the user to log in and access My OPSWAT Central Management resources successfully.

To find the cookies and session information stored by My OPSWAT Central Management:

1. Users should employ their browser’s Inspect/Inspect Element tool, which is accessible via right-click/ctrl-click menu, as illustrated in the screenshot below (Chrome, Safari).

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/gsx3rnzn9e86t4hybab0a1pwfmg2lflsfv9d1759swfj5artmydsphxhk08sd2n6.png)

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/hyojqh2ghj15bwr0ckid68wro6xs99sdr3eh146wb02fo89vo86qm3d66rb3hty3.png)

2. When the inspection panel opens, locate the **Cookies** and **Local Storage** sections, which are under the **Application>Storage** tab in Chrome and Microsoft Edge, and under the **Storage** tab in Safari, as illustrated below (Chrome, Safari).

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/9bt79irs00f3turbq8x8qw397p3xr9a9g53hjurkazs9ngne9xxt3zgbammw2zls.png)

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/y9qbqx1lqy5rvv2us20105p9s3gf08msytsa28g7vijhe8rrkx2mjt0bzw29u9d4.png)

Here, users can explore lists of all data held by My OPSWAT Central Management per session.
