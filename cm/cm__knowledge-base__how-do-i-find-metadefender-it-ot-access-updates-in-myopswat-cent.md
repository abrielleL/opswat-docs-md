# How do I find My OPSWAT Central Management updates in My OPSWAT emails?

> **Check Your Version:**
>
> This article is applied to My OPSWAT Central Management releases deployed on Windows and Linux systems.

**Issue:** Update emails for My OPSWAT Central Management contain references to many different OPSWAT products, which can be confusing for customers.

**Resolution:** To identify updates relevant to My OPSWAT Central Management, follow these steps:

- **Search the Email:** Look for the terms "Endpoint Security" or "Secure Access" within the update email.
- **Assess Relevance:** If either "Endpoint Security" or "Secure Access" appears prominently in the email, the update likely contains information relevant to MD IT Access.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/1yda/tsbl3cla6dtjw9mfezho01yjbsmds3hknm4gbcadnri4ugamttismxlvmbajpo41.png)

- **No Relevant Updates:** If neither term is found, it is likely that there are no major updates to MD IT Access functionality in that particular email.
