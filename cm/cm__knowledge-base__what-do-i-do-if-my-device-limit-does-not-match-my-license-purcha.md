# What do I do if my device limit does not match my license purchased?

Should you find that the device limit displayed in **Console>Settings>Global**, as illustrated below, does not correspond to your newly purchased device-limit increase, please follow the instructions below.

> **Pro tip:**
>
> The device count display (e.g., **2/51**) indicates your current device usage and limit:
>
> - **2** represents the number of devices currently being monitored.
> - **51** is the maximum number of devices allowed under your current license.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/dck7fomxzf9i8mdc6fpk5pr113frc02lo8jmv9u0zdzzo3ivxj6cdbkjn0apn960.png)

1. Log out of the **Console**.
2. Log back into the **Console**.

With the Console refreshed, you should now see an updated, true reflection of your device limit under **Console>Settings>Global**.

> **Sales:**
>
> For other devices-limit issues, or to **Add Additional Devices To Your License**, please contact the [OPSWAT Sales](https://www.opswat.com/contact) team.

> **Support:**
>
> If you have followed the instructions above but find that **Your Device Limit Is Still Not Corresponding To The License Your Purchased**, please open a [Support Case](https://my.opswat.com/support) with the OPSWAT team via phone, online chat.
