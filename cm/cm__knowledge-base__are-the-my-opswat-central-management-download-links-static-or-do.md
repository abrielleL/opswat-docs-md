# Are the My OPSWAT Central Management download links static or do they expire?

The My OPSWAT Central Management download link is static and will not expire. The URL is static and is formatted as follows:

- [https://gears.opswat.com/console/download/{your-metaaccess-api-key}](https://gears.opswat.com/console/download/%7Byour-metaaccess-api-key%7D)
