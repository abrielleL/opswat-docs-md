# Why am I not be able to grab the update package directly from OCM10 to manually upload to MetaDefender Core?

> **Check Your Version:**
>
> This article applies to all OPSWAT Central Management version 10.x

> Even though OPSWAT Central Management (OCM) 10 download the engine update packages from OPSWAT’s update server and store it locally on the OCM server, we cannot grab the update package directly from OCM10 to manually upload to MetaDefender Core. The files have been hashed and stored as multiple separate files.
