# Overview

This section explains how to manage policies in your account.

My OPSWAT Central Management groups policies of products by solution as below

1. Peripherial Media: policies for MetaDefender Kiosk
2. Endpoint Security: policies for endpoints
3. Hardware Supply Chain: policies for MetaDefender Drive
4. File Security: policies for MetaDefender Core

## Manage policies

1. Log into the My OPSWAT Central Management console.
2. Navigate to the **Policies,** select a solution you would like to view policies.
3. You can perform some actions on a specific policy, such as:

- **Clone** - lets you quickly create a duplicate of a policy within an account.
- **Delete** - allows you delete a policy.
- **Export Policy** allows you export details of a policy for auditing later.
- Create A new policy: create a new policy with a default policy
- Import a policy: from a file or an instance

## Create a new policy

My OPSWAT Central Management will create a default policy based on the latest version of a product.

1. Log into the My OPSWAT Central Management console.
2. Navigate to the **Policies,** select a solution you would like to create a new policy
3. Click the **CREATE NEW POLICY** button.
4. Fill in the **Policy Name** and **Description** (optional).
5. Click **SAVE.**
6. Enter your PIN to confirm the action if required

## Update a policy

1. Log into My OPSWAT Central Management console
2. Navigate to the **Policies,** select a solution you would like to view a new policy
3. Select a policy you wish to update.
4. Navigate to sections you would like to update.
5. Click on **SAVE** button and confirm your PIN to save changes.

## Import a policy

1. Log into the My OPSWAT Central Management console.
2. Navigate to the **Policies,** select a solution you would like to create a new policy
3. Click Import Policy from an instance/device
4. Select an instance/device to import that instance/device to a policy
5. Click Next to start importing

## Apply a policy to a group

1. Log into the My OPSWAT Central Management console.
2. Navigate to **Inventory > Groups.**
3. Click on a group you wish to apply the policy to, and navigate to the **Settings** tab.
4. Under **Assigned Policy** , select a desired policy you want to assign to the group.
5. Confirm your change with your PIN.

**Note that* My OPSWAT Central Management *will re-assess device status for devices in the group with the new policy, except changes for Threat Detection.*
