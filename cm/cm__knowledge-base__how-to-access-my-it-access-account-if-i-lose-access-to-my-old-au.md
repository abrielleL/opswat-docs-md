# How to access My OPSWAT Central Management account if I lose access to my old authenticator app or changed phones?

> **Check Your Version:**
>
> This article applies to all My OPSWAT releases on the Cloud

> **Requirements:**
>
> To follow these instructions you will need:
>
> 1. Access to the internet.
> 2. Access to your Reset code setup during MFA enrollment.

Please follow the instructions below to reset your MFA and access your account:

1. Make sure you have access to your reset code from your initial MDA enrollment. This was the second step during enrollment as shown in the example below:

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/1yda/6hpb8bnags2u2rqc0d5ftdsiub5xoj5vu61jxcodgg82r0icf6uy91hvryoxztqo.png)

2. To use this code, you will have to enter your email address at my.opswat.com and sign in. When prompted for TOTP code, select “User recovery code”.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/1yda/vz9c9165mefqwkr905s62cw88tb68zx6g72bzjlt9k2q0oa2em6g486kwqpvf60v.png)

3. Enter your recovery code. After this, you will be redirected to MyOPSWAT portal screen.
4. At this point, you should be able to go to [gears.opswat.com](http://gears.opswat.com/) and access your account without the need to enter an TOTP code.

> **Important:**
>
> Please keep in mind that depending on your organizations policies, you will be required to enable MFA again.

> **Support:**
>
> If **Further Assistance** is required, please proceed to log a [**support case or chatting with our support engineer**](https://my.opswat.com/portal/support).
