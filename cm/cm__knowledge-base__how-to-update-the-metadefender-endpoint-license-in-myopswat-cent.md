# How to update the MetaDefender Endpoint license in MyOPSWAT Central Management v10 On-Premises?

> **Check Your Version:**
>
> This article applies to all MyOPSWAT Central Management v10 releases deployed on Windows and Linux systems.

## Purpose

This article explains how to **update the MetaDefender Endpoint license from MyOPSWAT Central Management v10 On-Premises** in both an **online** and **offline** environment. This is done when the license undergoes modifications to the **number of managed devices** or when adding **new packages** to the license.

## Steps

1. **Open MyOPSWAT Central Management** WEB Portal.
2. **Navigate to** **License** on the left hand side menu.
3. Under MetaDefender Endpoint, on the right hand sand of the screen, **click on the 3 dots**.
4. **Select Update**.
5. Under Add MetaDefender Endpoint License choose a license activation method: **Online or Offline**.
   1. For **Online activation**, simply use your license key to update the instance.
   2. For **Offline activation**, the instance will have to be activated by using MyOPSWAT Portal. See the procedure here: [https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/license-management/activate-license](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/license-management/activate-license)
