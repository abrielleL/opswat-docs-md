# Why does OPSWAT Mobile App need permissions to read/write on my storage/photo/files?

> **Check Your Product:**
>
> This article applies to all OPSWAT Mobile App releases deployed on Android and iOS devices.

The OPSWAT Mobile App uses the **storage/photo/files (if present)/internal** storage folder on your mobile device to store temporary data regarding the device’s security and health.

OPSWAT confirms that the OPSWAT Mobile App will neither read nor modify any other data within your **storage/photo/files (if present)/internal** storage folder.
