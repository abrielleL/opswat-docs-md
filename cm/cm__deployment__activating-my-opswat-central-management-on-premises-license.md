# Activating My OPSWAT Central Management On-Premises License

To activate a product license for My OPSWAT Central Management On-Premises

Navigate to Settings > Global > License in the sidebar menu.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/5hicapdyud2tquoeu31sdzzna1it10n195svkgzpe5i5nzhthmxzcik7k61zi65u.png)

- **Online Activation**: The My OPSWAT Central Management On-Premises will contact the OPSWAT License portal to activate a license for a selected product based on your **Activation Key** and the **Deployment ID**.
- **Offline Activation:** You can manually activate your activation key for your My OPSWAT Central Management On-Premises server on [My OPSWAT](https://my.opswat.com/portal/products) then upload a license file to your server.

## Online Activation

To activate the product with this option, the below requirements must be met:

- The server has an Internet connection and can connect to OPSWAT License portal ([https://activation.dl.opswat.com/](https://activation.dl.opswat.com/))
- You have a valid activation key for My OPSWAT Central Management On-Premises

With Internet connection, My OPSWAT Central Management On-Premises can be activated directly using the **Activation key** received upon purchasing the product. Perform the following steps for an online activation:

In the **License** tab, choose **Online** Activation.

1. Fill the **Activation Key** field with your key.
2. Click the **ACTIVATE** button to activate.

If your license is valid, the product shall be activated with number of devices limit as you purchased. Otherwise, you will get an error message.

## Offline Activation

You can activate a license with this option if you have the valid **Deployment ID** of the My OPSWAT Central Management On-Premises instance and the **Activation Key** received upon purchasing the product or a trial key.

Please follow the steps detailed below to acquire the activation file containing the unlock key required for offline activation.

1. Log into [My OPSWAT](https://my.opswat.com/portal/products)
2. Click Activate License in the left sidebar menu.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/58gi9k9j9qvzkywoa4e6utdiru2tok3inwy02nu3jkyfee3qigolo32xbrwyxfwm.png)

3. In the pop-up dialog, select the corresponding product for the **Product** field.
   - My OPSWAT Central Management On-Premises

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/v1ynbd8afhwgwb8e11xiw4xibgychte1wppeksn7feuskvkoi21o818i078gkjsf.png)

4. Enter your activation key and your My OPSWAT Central Management On-Premises deployment ID
5. Click the Activate button.
6. Click **Download** in the next dialog to download the activation file.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/oxpwlpde60k3o9a0abfye79ub2h6eq3uag8crbvc966q2em5j4qbiw19fc1ntty6.png)

7. Go back to the My OPSWAT Central Management On-Premises console, select offline mode in the Activation file, choose the activation file, and click the ACTIVATE button

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/gn0bl2gr3bd9do090ph8gbmj1lkvv34m17u2patly2ohdj9guy90cgvefqy869pc.png)
