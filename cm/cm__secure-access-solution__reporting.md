# Reporting

MetaDefender NAC Cloud offers customizable reporting features, allowing organizations to tailor reports to their specific requirements. Administrators can easily add or remove sections, define groups, and set timeframes to generate personalized reports. This flexibility enables administrators to quickly access relevant data at a glance, empowering them to make informed decisions.

## Report Manager - Customizing your Report

The **Report Manager** section within the Reporting tool is available for administrators to review and access their customized reports. Actions such as **Edit, Clone, Delete** are available by selecting the (⋮) by a specific report. Administrators can also choose to activate or deactivate reports based on their needs.

1. Navigate to **Settings > Reports > Report Manager > Create A New Report**
2. Fill out **Name** and select the desired **Device Groups**.
3. Select the **Report Type** as **RADIUS NAC**.
4. Choose the **Scope** of the report. Options are Daily, Weekly, Monthly.
5. Input the list of users and emails that should receive the report.
6. Lastly, enter the **Message** that will be attached with the report when sent to designated users.

To review reports created in My OPSWAT Central Management, administrators can use the **Filters** available such as **Recurrence** and **Report Type**. This will assist administrators in locating the desired reports within in the **Report Manager**.
