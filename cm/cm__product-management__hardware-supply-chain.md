# Hardware Supply Chain

The **Hardware Supply Chain dashboard** provides a comprehensive overview of the security status of the hardware supply chain. It includes key metrics, trends, geographical distribution of detections, and detailed assessments. The dashboard highlights the number of issues detected, the health status of MetaDefender Drives, and detailed information about the hosts involved in the scans. This page helps users understand the effectiveness of their scanning activities, identify areas that need attention, and ensure the security and health of their systems.

How to Access You can access the dashboard from the left navigation menu by going to **Dashboard > Hardware Supply Chain**.

### **Scanning statistic**

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/xaknn2lo3usxpgr6i7hhqq4dzw494jupxpepps7mga4iz5f20byqm3bqjxx7888b.png)

- **Scan Sessions:** The total number of scans conducted.
- **Total Files with Issues:** The number of files identified with issues.
- **MetaDefender Drives:** The number of MetaDefender Drives involved in the scanning process.
- **Processed Files:** The total number of files scanned by MetaDefender Drives.
- **Total Scan Duration:** The cumulative time spent on all scanning sessions.
