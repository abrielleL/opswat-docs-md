# What are the guidelines for the OPSWAT Endpoint Security Certification program?

> **OPSWAT Endpoint Security Certification program**
>
> The OPSWAT Endpoint Security Certification program assesses and certifies endpoint security products for compatibility, effectiveness, and interoperability with major access control solutions. It ensures that security applications meet industry standards, reducing security risks and operational issues such as device blockages and support escalations. See more: [Endpoint Security Certification - Ensure Your Product is Enterprise Ready - OPSWAT](https://www.opswat.com/partners/certification).

Belows are steps to assess your endpoint security applications with OPSWAT Endpoint Security Certification:

#### Step 1: Contact Your Vendor

Reach out to your current endpoint security application vendor's support channels.

E.g. [Microsoft Community](https://feedbackportal.microsoft.com/feedback/)

#### Step 2: Request to get certified with OPSWAT certification program

Express your desire for the vendor to assess their products and solutions with OPSWAT Endpoint Security Certification program.

Certified products receive a certification badge, providing reassurance of their reliability and performance in enterprise environments, marketing opportunities, and inclusion in OPSWAT’s resources.
