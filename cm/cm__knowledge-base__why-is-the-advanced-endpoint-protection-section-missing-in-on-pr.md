# Why is the Advanced Endpoint Protection section missing in on-prem My OPSWAT Central Management?

> **Check Your Version:** This article applies to all OPSWAT Central Management releases deployed on Windows and Linux systems.

**Issue:** The Advanced Endpoint Protection section is missing in the on-prem version of OPSWAT Central Management.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/h6qbty04ha1zjauc4p0yqdzqz1xx5fg116qqntzs71h6cedyfigiccovefzf4oor.png)

**Resolution:**

The Advanced Endpoint Protection section is available in the cloud version of OPSWAT Central Management under `Policies -> Endpoint Security`. You can then choose an existing policy or create a new one. Within your selected policy, navigate to `Advanced Endpoint Protection -> Enable Threat Detection for Windows/Linux/MacOS`.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/annzydv0nwuu60fazhmziftssueo8zdn851456krf7vlejrcug8gljm0ff9cxuji.png)

 

For the on-prem version, please verify that your license includes Advanced Endpoint Protection by contacting your account manager or sales representative.
