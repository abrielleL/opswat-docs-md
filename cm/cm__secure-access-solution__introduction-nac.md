# Introduction

## What is MetaDefender NAC v10?

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/uxwcaolsaw3mgssizg3l4zyxb7rw3qervcpehijgwxbj49vwop7je7pf412oznmi.png)

MetaDefender NAC v10 is what we call the set a features in My OPSWAT Central Management that allow it to fill the role of a NAC. Like MetaDefender NAC v8, MetaDefender NAC v10 can be used to dynamically assign devices on your network to different VLANs, apply ACLs, or enforce other low-level networking controls based on policies defined in a feature-rich admin console. Unlike  MetaDefender NAC v8, with MetaDefender NAC v10 everything is managed from the My OPSWAT Central Management console, either from a self-hosted deployment or the cloud-hosted version maintained by OPSWAT.

Looking for the MetaDefender NAC v8 docs? Those can be found [here](https://www.opswat.com/docs/manac).

Interested in migrating your v8 deployment to v10? Take a look at [Migrating from MD NAC v8 to v10](https://www.opswat.com/docs/cm/secure-access-solution/nac-policy-logic--v8-vs-v10) guideline.
