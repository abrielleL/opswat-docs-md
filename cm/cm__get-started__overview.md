### What is My OPSWAT Central Management?

My OPSWAT Central Management is a centralized management and orchestration platform designed to unify OPSWAT product deployments. It replaces fragmented, instance-by-instance management with a Single-Pane-of-Glass architecture.

By aggregating data from all connected MetaDefender products across cloud, on-premises, and air-gapped environments, My OPSWAT Central Management provides the visibility needed to monitor health status, strengthen security posture, and execute real-time remediation from a unified interface.

**Key Capabilities**

- **Centralized Product Management:** Remotely manage diverse OPSWAT products, streamline data flows visibility, and aggregate cross-product data for unified reporting.
- **Endpoint Security Control and Management:** Manage endpoint devices and enforce security posture checks to ensure synchronization with organizational policies and regulatory standards.
- **Real-Time Threat Visibility & Risk Monitoring**: Eliminate security blind spots by centralizing data from all connected products for faster threat detection and vulnerability response.
- **Consistent Policy Enforcement**: Deploy standardized security rules across global MetaDefender networks to eliminate configuration drifts in distributed environments.
- **Secure Access Control**: Protect critical networks and applications by enforcing strict identity verification and application-layer access controls.
- **Operational Efficiency**: Minimize administrative overhead through centralized license tracking, Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), and 24/7 remote monitoring.

**Who Is This For?**

My OPSWAT Central Management is built to support a variety of critical security and operational roles:

1. **IT Security Administrator**: Technical operators focused on access control, system configuration, and policy enforcement.
2. **CISO/CSO/CIO**: Strategic leaders committed to safeguarding assets, mitigating risk, and proving compliance.
3. **SOC Manager & Analyst**: Incident response teams prioritizing real-time threat detection and forensic analysis.
4. **System Integrators & MSPs (Managed Service Providers)**: Service providers managing multi-client deployments and scaling operations via APIs.
5. **Compliance & Risk Officers**: Audit-focused roles ensuring adherence to standards like NIST, CISA, and GDPR.
6. **Organizations protecting Critical Infrastructure**: Regulated powerhouse in Energy, Finance, Manufacturing, Government, requiring centralized control over distributed global sites.
