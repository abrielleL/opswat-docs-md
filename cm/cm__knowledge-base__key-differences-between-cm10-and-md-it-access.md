# Key Differences Between My OPSWAT Central Management and MetaDefender IT Access

We are excited to introduce an enhanced version of MetaDefender IT Access: **My OPSWAT Central Management**. It is built upon the great features of all all predecessors, MetaDefender IT Access and previous Central Management platform, and brings several enhancements in performance, security, and management capabilities.

Here’s a comparison to help you understand the key differences between My OPSWAT Central Management and MetaDefender IT Access:

**1. Centralized Product Management**

- **MetaDefender IT Access**: only manage MetaDefender Endpoint and MetaDefender OT Access.
- **My OPSWAT Central Management:** Enjoy **centralized product management**, allowing you to manage all OPSWAT products and solutions from a single pane of glass for improved efficiency.

**2. Enhanced Dashboards and Reports**

- **MetaDefender IT Access:** The reporting and dashboard features were limited in secure access, device compliance and endpoint security.
- **My OPSWAT Central Management**: Benefit from **enhanced dashboards and reporting capabilities**, offering a global view for solutions that you deployed. You can now easily visualize security risks and trends to make more informed decisions.

**3. License Management**

- **MetaDefender IT Access**: only limited to endpoints.
- My OPSWAT Central Management: With the **new version**, license management is now fully integrated, giving you an easier way to track and manage licenses for OPSWAT products directly within the platform.

**4. Streamlined Update Processes**

- **My OPSWAT Central Management:** Enjoy a **streamlined update process** for products and engines that doesn't require Internet connection, ensuring you always have the latest features and security enhancements with minimal effort.

**5. Performance and Security Improvements**

- **My OPSWAT Central Management:** This release includes significant **performance and security enhancements**, ensuring faster operations, reduced downtime, and stronger protection for your infrastructure.

## **Why Upgrade?**

Upgrading to My OPSWAT Central Management will help you unlock these advanced features, improve the efficiency of your product management, and ensure your infrastructure remains secure and optimized. The new version is designed to provide you with better control, visibility, and convenience as you manage your solutions.

As we transition to My OPSWAT Central Management, [MetaDefender IT Access console](MetaDefender IT Access console) will only be available for a limited time. After login, MetaDefender IT Access customers can switch to My OPSWAT Central Management console without losing any data to experience the new console and provide feedback. The customers can switch back to MetaDefender IT Access console while getting used to the new console at any time.

If you’re ready to experience the benefits of the new version, we encourage you to switch to My OPSWAT Central Management today and take advantage of these advanced features. Should you need any assistance, please contact our support team.
