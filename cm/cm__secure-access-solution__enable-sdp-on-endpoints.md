# Enable SDP on Endpoint

Administrators can enable SDP on end-user devices by following one of two methods.

## Solution 1: Enabling SDP globally

Administrators have the option to turn on SDP for all devices within their My OPSWAT Central Management account.

To enable SDP globally:

1. Log into My OPSWAT Central Management console
2. Navigate to **Settings > Global > Endpoint Clients**
3. Check off **Enable Secure IT Access for all devices**
4. Save the changes and enter your PIN to confirm

Within a few minutes after your change, the MetaDefender Endpoint will check in with the My OPSWAT Central Management backend servers, note the change, and begin installing SDP for the client devices.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/g0mx6qwmkhx8sw0yapmwz87sur7tif7kcimlplmgiqz3wh2tdazq7tfwf0zoudps.png)

## Solution 2: Enabling SDP per device group

Administrators can gradually turn on SDP on your organization by utilizing group overrides.

To enable SDP on a device group:

1. Log into My OPSWAT Central Management console
2. Navigate to **Inventory > Groups**
3. Select a group you would like to enable SDP, and select the **Settings** tab
4. Toggle on the **Secure Access** section, and check off **Enable Secure IT Access for this group...**

Within a few minutes after your change, the MetaDefender Endpoint will check in with the My OPSWAT Central Management backend servers, note the change, and begin installing SDP for the client devices.
