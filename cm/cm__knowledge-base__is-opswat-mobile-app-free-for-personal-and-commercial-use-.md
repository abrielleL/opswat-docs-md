# Is OPSWAT Mobile App free for personal and commercial use?

> **Check Your Product:**
>
> This article applies to all OPSWAT Mobile App releases deployed on Android and iOS devices.

Yes, the OPSWAT Mobile app is free for both personal and commercial use - but not for resale.

Centralized My OPSWAT Central Management is also available for remote management of up to 50 devices. If you would like to manage more than 50 devices, please contact the OPSWAT Sales team via the link below.

For more about My OPSWAT Central Management, please [Read This](https://www.opswat.com/products/metaaccess).
