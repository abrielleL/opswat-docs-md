# What is the latest My OPSWAT Central Management version?

> **Check Your Version:** 
>
> This article applies to all My OPSWAT Central Management releases deployed on Windows and Linux systems. 

 

Browse My OPSWAT Central Management [Release Notes](https://www.opswat.com/docs/cm/release-notes)* *for details on the latest version,* *version history, version comparisons and release notes. 

 

Alternatively, go directly through the [My OPSWAT](https://my.opswat.com/portal) for a list of available downloads.: 

1. Login to My OPSWAT portal 

2. Click on “My OPSWAT Central Management On-premises”

3. Click on “Download” button

> **Pro Tip: **  
>
> OPSWAT highly recommends that users upgrade to the latest version of My OPSWAT Central Management. Current versions incorporate state-of-the-art features and necessary bug fixes, and are freely available for all license holders to download.
