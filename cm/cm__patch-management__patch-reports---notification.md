# Patch Reports & Notifications

Patch Reports & Notifications provides administrators with the centralized tools to maintain full visibility into patch compliance, communicate patch posture to stakeholders, and receive timely alerts for critical patching events, all from within My OPSWAT Central Management.

## Choose what you need

| Features | Details |
| ---- | ---- |
| [Custom Patch Reports](#custom-patch-reports) | Generate a shareable, point-in-time record of patch compliance, on a schedule or on demand |
| [Patch Notification](#patch-notification) | Get alerted immediately the moment a deployment finishes, fails, or a new patch shows up |
| [Patch SLA](#patch-sla) | Define maximum remediation timeframes per severity level and track compliance status |
