# Can I monitor or manage mobile devices from My OPSWAT Central Management?

> **Check Your Product:**
>
> This article applies to the current My OPSWAT Central Management Console and all OPSWAT Mobile App releases deployed on Android and iOS devices.

Yes, **My OPSWAT Central Management** offers intuitive, centralized monitoring and management of a range of device types, including:

- Desktops
- Laptops
- Servers
- Virtual Machines
- Tablets
- Smartphones

Additionality, **My OPSWAT Central Management** supports a diverse range of popular operating systems, including:

- Windows
- macOS
- Linux
- Android
- iOS

To adjust policy aspects specific to mobile devices, please go to your **My OPSWAT Central Management** **Console>Policy Management>Policies>Relevant Policy>Mobile Devices** tab, as illustrated below.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/1yda/ykzflgkfme1eahry81a6ssinnpno06xy28462xol6bg69oda5m5mxovsuwlkgm11.png)

> **Pro Tip:**
>
> Your free My OPSWAT Central Management account will allow for monitoring and management of an inventory of up to 50 devices across this full range, including mobile devices (tablets and smartphones).
>
> For detailed instructions on how to Enroll Android and iOS devices to your My OPSWAT Central Management account, please see [How do I register a mobile device to a My OPSWAT Central Management account?](https://www.opswat.com/docs/cm/knowledge-base/how-do-i-register-a-mobile-device-to-a-metadefender-it-access-ac)
