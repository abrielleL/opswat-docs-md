# Applications

MetaDefender Endpoint detects applications installed on devices and reports back to My OPSWAT Central Management.

From My OPSWAT Central Management, administrators can view all applications installed on devices in their organization.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/c017f9u0kc6lc1vn41de0et3sc56xpatjvl99994h2eox4azrc5n8d4uk10krxbl.png)

Administrators have a detailed view on a specific application such as what devices installed that application, vulnerabilities associated to it
