# How could I manage another account?

> **Pro Tip:**
>
> To manage endpoint products and view reports of other accounts, an owner or administrator of that account must send you an invitation. If you do not not have an OPSWAT Account, sign up a free account [here](https://id.opswat.com/login).

## How to accept/decline an invitation

1. Log into the **My OPSWAT Central Management Console**.
2. Click your **Name** in the top right-hand corner of the screen, select **Manage Account** to access your **User Profile**.
3. Under the **Managed Account** tab, click **Accept**/**Decline** alongside the relevant account, in order to accept or decline the invitation, as illustrated below.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/lxfnd2rp4bk1hcvom1igvyr2qltatzo5sd9h64sw4vqu5tiiphu9u12dv7b8pxz3.png)

4. Once you have accepted the **Invitation**, you will be free to switch to the other account in order to view account information and/or, if you have been granted administrator privileges, to configure policies and manage devices via that account.

> **Pro Tip:**
>
> Some accounts may require you enter a PIN to confirm your changes when performing **Actions** or adjusting policy configurations and other setting for security verification. This **PIN** can be set up via your **My OPSWAT Central Management** **Console>User Profile>User Settings**, as illustrated in the screenshot below.
>
> Once your security **PIN** has been set up, it can be changed, disabled, or reset in the event that it is forgotten.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/iandedw2vca8haa5jy8f8p0fhh1e0uzsq19tifxqi2qoxr1ifwfk2mue0x8ttwau.png)

## How to switch to another account

1. Log into the **My OPSWAT Central Management Console**.
2. Click your **Name** in the top right-hand corner of the screen, select **Manage Account** to access your **User Profile**.
3. Under the **Managed Account** tab, click the  **setting** icon on the far, right-hand side of the account to which you’d like to switch, as illustrated below.
4. Click on the **Switch** button.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/x4q0ib44479i8c6o2wr54qbe5mw10loi621jpvz9un6ipf74h08ntcsplv2oisb8.png)

## How to set up an account as the default account

> **Pro Tip:**
>
> My OPSWAT Central Management offers you the option to set one of your managed accounts as the default.
>
> Once you’ve set a particular account as the default, you’ll automatically be landed into that account each time you log into the My OPSWAT Central Management Console, with no further action needed.

1. Log into the **My OPSWAT Central Management Console**.
2. Click your **Name** in the top right-hand corner of the screen, select **Manage Account** to access your **User Profile**.
3. Under the **Managed Account** tab, click the **Setting** icon on the far, right-hand side of the account that you’d like to set as default, as illustrated below.
4. Select the option **Make default**.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/92vx93l99jtco46huruou6458cg52ai8jtcl0dtkmxr2ccfjy1mnvy2b736f4ax6.png)
