# What is the Session Shield End of Life?

> **Check Your Product:**
>
> This article applies to all My OPSWAT Central Management products deployed on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS systems.

No, the OPSWAT Session Shield is no longer available as a feature in My OPSWAT Central Management or any other OPSWAT product - as of 9th June 2015.

The executable files for Session Shield have been removed, and the privacy proxy has been stopped.
