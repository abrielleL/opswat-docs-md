# User Profile

> **Info**
>
> This documentation is for console users only. It does not apply to SDP end-users within My OPSWAT Central Management.

To access your profile page, select your name in the top right-hand corner of My OPSWAT Central Management, then select Manage Account menu, click User Settings tab.

## Update Name

On your user profile page, click the button **Update Your Profile** under your name, you will be redirected to OPSWAT account profile page to update your name.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/o5fmkefis4pxsz2136pk5197bmkxmh1uoco4t4vycglgkvqrxmedff0jdpwnc14c.png)

## PIN Security

Users needs to enter their PIN to make changes on accounts that enables PIN requirements.

### Setup your PIN

1. Log into your **My OPSWAT Central Management Console** and click your account **Name** in the top, right-hand corner of the screen, select **Management account** menu to access your **User Profile**.
2. Under the **User Settings** tab, enter your New **PIN** and **Confirm New PIN**.
3. Then, click **Save**, to setup your PIN as illustrated below.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/mynufakyuv0ks6ljdz1fl2e26bnvwadguruwezqgki8j8jnx08634xnujvukcgpj.png)

### Change your PIN

1. Log into your **My OPSWAT Central Management Console** and click your account **Name** in the top, right-hand corner of the screen, select **Management account** menu to access your **User Profile**.
2. Under the **User Settings** tab, enter your **Current PIN** and **New PIN** in the fields provided.
3. Then, click **Update**, as illustrated below.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/riqtywmcjz5gyge6az1cyf251uvz4ahht4n5rvrntzeb8ci5yr423yi90mktx98z.png)

### Reset your PIN

> **Pro Tip:**
>
> Should you forget your security **PIN**, you are free to request a temporary PIN at any time, which will be generated and sent to you via email, for immediate use.

1. Log into your **My OPSWAT Central Management Console** and click your account **Name** in the top, right-hand corner of the screen, select **Management account** menu to access your **User Profile**.
2. Under the **User Settings** tab, click **Forgot your PIN?**, as illustrated below.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/r5n93c3aqy44xuqlusj0ajm7izyhadf1d8yxoioreyiqn05h708dxi1i04m2a348.png)

3. In the pop-up window, confirm your decision to request a temporary PIN by clicking **Reset**, as illustrated below.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/97qW/8m4rgsbt7o9448rhc92sxk6we5m2jrjqi7mklct61b3agmi38hznp71c4qoeszgj.png)

> **Support:**
>
> If you have followed the instructions above but are having difficulty **Changing Your Security PIN**, please open a [Support Case](https://my.opswat.com/support) with the OPSWAT team via phone, online chat or form, or feel free to ask the community on our [OPSWAT Expert Forum](https://go2.opswat.com/s).
