# How can I customize remediation pages for a group?

> **Check Your Product:**
>
> This article applies to versions of My OPSWAT Central Management Console subsequent to V1.3.3, with managed Client deployments on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS systems.

My OPSWAT Central Management now empowers administrators to fully customize device issue **Remediation Pages** with their organization’s own branding and text.

This feature allows for distinct customizations per user group, further enhancing the endpoint user experience and meeting OPSWAT customer demands for maximum localization.

For detailed instructions on how to customize remediation pages for your user groups, please [Read This](https://www.opswat.com/docs/cm/configurations/endpoint-remediation-page).
