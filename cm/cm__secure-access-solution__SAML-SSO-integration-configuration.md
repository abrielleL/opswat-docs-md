# Configuration

If your organization already uses Single Sign On (SSO) services for user authentication before accessing your applications/services, you can integrate with My OPSWAT Central Management to prevent non-compliant devices from accessing your applications/resources. We support most Identity Providers (IdPs) who support the Security Assertion Markup Language ( SAML) protocol (such as Okta, PingIdentity, Centrify, and OneLogin) as well as applications/services that support SAML SSO.

With My OPSWAT Central Management, you no longer need to invest in expensive infrastructure or resources for upgrading, configuring, and maintaining your existing solution.

**To integrate your existing SSO system with OPSWAT solution, here is a check list :**

- [Enable Secure Access on My OPSWAT Central Management account](https://www.opswat.com/docs/cm/secure-access-solution/enable-SAML-SSO-enable-secure-access)
- [Add Protected Applications with IdP Method](https://www.opswat.com/docs/cm/secure-access-solution/SAML-SSO-add-protected-applications)
- [Configure Access Rules](https://www.opswat.com/docs/cm/secure-access-solution/SAML-SSO-configure-access-rules)
- [Update Applications Settings on Identity Provider](https://www.opswat.com/docs/cm/secure-access-solution/update-applications-settings-on-identity-provider)
- [Configure SSO Settings on Applications](https://www.opswat.com/docs/cm/secure-access-solution/configure-sso-settings-on-applications)

All the above steps need to be carried to get the solution to work. Skipping any of these may cause some login issues. We strongly recommend to follow [Test Your Integration](https://www.opswat.com/docs/cm/secure-access-solution/test-your-integration) guideline.

We also provide detailed guidelines on how integrate specific IdP and applications in [Sample Guidelines.](https://www.opswat.com/docs/cm/secure-access-solution/sample-guidelines)
