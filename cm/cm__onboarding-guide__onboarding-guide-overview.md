# Onboarding Guide Overview

This onboarding guide is designed to help you set up and operate My OPSWAT Central Management safely, efficiently, and with minimal disruption. It is intended to help you understand the platform at a high level and prepare your environment for centralized security operations.

Refer to this guide during initial deployment or when you need to review your setup. Once onboarding is complete, My OPSWAT Central Management functions as a centralized control for monitoring, policy enforcement, and operational oversight across your security landscape.
