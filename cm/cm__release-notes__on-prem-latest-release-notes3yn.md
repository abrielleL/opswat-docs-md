# Release Notes for v10.0.2506

### Release Date: June 30, 2025

Version: 10.0.2506

## Key Capabilities

### 1.    Unified Management

Get real-time insights into the health status, compliance, and security risks detected from all enrolled devices on a single platform. Enforce endpoint security policies, with a global executive view of data processing, and detailed device usage and access patterns.

### 2.    Secure Access

Ensure that access from devices to your cloud and legacy applications is secure, whether connecting from the office, home, or other remote locations. Available as Secure Cloud Access with SAML IdP Integration.

### 3.    Centralized Policy Management

Create, deploy, and update security policies at scale, improving your organization’s compliance posture.

### 4.    Streamlined Product Updates

Push critical updates across all OPSWAT products in both online and offline modes to stay ahead of evolving threats, with no manual intervention required.

### 5.    Granular Role-Based Access Control

Assign roles and permissions tailored to your organizational structure, ensuring operational transparency and security.

### 6.    Audit-Ready Logging and Reporting

Generate detailed logs and compliance reports with just a few clicks, which can be used for audits and regulatory reviews.
