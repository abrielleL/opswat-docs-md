# How do I change my PIN?

> **Check Your Product:**
>
> This article applies to the current My OPSWAT Central Management, and all licensed accounts.

Please refer [this guideline](https://www.opswat.com/docs/cm/user-management/user-profile) to update/reset your PIN.

> **Support:**
>
> If you have followed the instructions above but are having difficulty **Changing Your Security PIN**, please open a [Support Case](https://my.opswat.com/support) with the OPSWAT team via phone, online chat or form, or feel free to ask the community on our [OPSWAT Expert Forum](https://go2.opswat.com/s).
