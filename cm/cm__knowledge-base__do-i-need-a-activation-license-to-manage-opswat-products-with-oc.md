# Do I need a activation license to manage OPSWAT products with OCM v10?

OPSWAT OCM v10 (on prem) does not need a license key activation (like our other products) to function successfully and be able to manage other OPSWAT products.

The only license necessary on the OCM deployment is related to the “MetaDefender Endpoint” tab and it is necessary in events where endpoint management is performed from OCM.
