# Product Rebranding

Introducing My OPSWAT Central Management – The advanced version of MetaDefender IT Access

We are pleased to unveil My OPSWAT Central Management, the enhanced and rebranded version of MetaDefender IT Access. My OPSWAT Central Management is a single pane of glass platform to manage OPSWAT products and solutions, collect real-time data, and generate comprehensive reports. It also enables administrators to perform operations globally, manage licenses, and simplify product updates. My OPSWAT Central Management streamlines your operations while enhancing visibility and control to safeguard your critical infrastructure.

This enhancement marks the evolution of My OPSWAT Central Management into a next-generation central management platform, officially replacing all previous versions of "Central Management" introduced by OPSWAT.

In this platform, customers can now manage multiple OPSWAT solutions including:

- OPSWAT Peripheral Media Protection – It prevents threat from external devices like USB drives and other removable media and ensures to inspect, sanitize, and secure data before it enters your infrastructure.
- OPSWAT Secure Access - By integrating zero-trust principles, it enforces strict security controls for remote and on-premise access, ensuring only compliant and trusted devices connect to critical environments.
- OPSWAT File Security – It offers advanced threat prevention by leveraging multiple technologies like multi-scanning, Content Disarm and Reconstruction (CDR), and Data Loss Prevention (DLP) to detect and prevent sophisticated known and unknown threats.
- OPSWAT Hardware Supply Chain Protection – It protects critical infrastructure by ensuring that hardware devices are free from cyber threats before they are integrated into operational environments.
- OPSWAT Endpoint Security - It ensures that USB drives and other removable media are scanned and sanitized before granting access, thereby preventing potential malware infiltration.

My OPSWAT Central Management retains the foundational architecture, security controls, and operational policies established under MetaDefender IT Access, including the ISO 27001 certified Information Security Management System and SOC 2 Type II audited security controls.

The MetaDefender IT Access version 3.5x.0 will remain available for a limited time before being fully transitioned. To ensure a seamless upgrade, all existing data and configurations from Metadefender IT Access version 3.5x.0 is available on My OPSWAT Central Management, allowing users to continue managing their security operations without disruption.

We encourage you to switch to My OPSWAT Central Management today and take advantage of these advanced features. You have the flexibility to explore My OPSWAT Central Management while retaining the option to revert to the previous version if needed.
