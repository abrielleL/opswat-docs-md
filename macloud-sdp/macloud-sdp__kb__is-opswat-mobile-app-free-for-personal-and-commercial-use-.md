# Is OPSWAT Mobile App free for personal and commercial use?

This article applies to all OPSWAT Mobile App releases deployed on Android and iOS devices.

Yes, the OPSWAT Mobile app is free for both personal and commercial use - but not for resale.

Centralized MetaDefender IT Access is also available for remote management of up to 50 devices. If you would like to manage more than 50 devices, please contact the OPSWAT Sales team via the link below.

For more about MetaDefender IT Access, please [Read This](https://www.opswat.com/products/metaaccess).

Sales:

For orders or further queries regarding the **OPSWAT** **MetaDefender IT Access** **Licensing**, please contact the [OPSWAT Sales](https://www.opswat.com/contact) team.

Support:

For queries, concerns or issues regarding **The OPSWAT Mobile App And** **MetaDefender IT Access**, please open a [Support Case](https://my.opswat.com/portal/support) with the OPSWAT team via phone, online chat or form, or feel free to ask the community on our [OPSWAT Expert Forum](https://go2.opswat.com/s).
