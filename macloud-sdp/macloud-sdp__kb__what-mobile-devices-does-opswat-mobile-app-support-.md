# What mobile devices does OPSWAT Mobile App support?

This article applies to all OPSWAT Mobile App releases deployed on Android and iOS devices.

The OPSWAT Mobile app is available for:

- **Smartphones**
- **Tablets**
- **iPhones**
- **iPads**

The OPSWAT Mobile App is not yet available for:

- **Smart Watches**
- **Android TVs**
- **Apple Watches**
- **Apple TVs**
- **Windows Phone**
- **Blackberry**

Supported operating systems include:

- **Android V7.0 and subsequent**
- **iOS V13.0 and subsequent**

Support:

For queries, concerns or issues regarding **The OPSWAT Mobile App’s Supported Devices**, please open a [Support Case](https://my.opswat.com/portal/support) with the OPSWAT team via phone, online chat or form, or feel free to ask the community on our [OPSWAT Expert Forum](https://go2.opswat.com/s).
