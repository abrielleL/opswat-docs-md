# How do I increase the score of Firewall on Security Score Windows devices?

**How to get max score**

**Step 1**: Open MetaDefender Endpoint and Refresh the data.

**Step 2:** If Firewall is not enabled → open Windows Settings.

**Step 3**: Step in “Privacy and security” tab → select “Firewall & network protection”

**Step 4:** Click turn on to all network that is currently off

**Step 5:** Back to MetaDefender Endpoint and refresh new score
