# What features of MetaDefender IT Access are available in on cloud deployment?

This article applies to the most updated releases deployed on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android, and Chrome systems.

The **MetaDefender IT Access** solution offers the flexibility of on cloud deployment. This article will provide you with comprehensive information on the available capabilities and supported platforms for on cloud deployment.
