# How do I log into the MetaDefender IT Access Console?

This article applies to all licensed MetaDefender IT Access products deployed on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS systems.

Please Note:

When you initially register an OPSWAT account, you will be asked to provide a username/email and password.

You will use these credentials to log into the [OPSWAT Portal](http://portal.opswat.com), where you will be free to download and use all free products or products purchased under your license.

These credentials will also be used to log into the unique management Consoles associated with your various OPSWAT products, including your MetaDefender IT Access Console.

To log into your MetaDefender IT Access Console, follow the instructions below:

1. Open your web browser and navigate to [https://gears.opswat.com](https://gears.opswat.com/).
2. You will be redirected to a MetaDefender IT Access login screen, where you can enter your OPSWAT account **Username/Email** and **Password**, as illustrated in the screenshot below.
3. Once you’ve entered your credentials, click the **Sign In** button.

4. Should you not be a registered OPSWAT account holder, the **MetaDefender IT Access Console** will be inaccessible to you until you have registered a free OPSWAT account.

To do so, please click on the **Register** link on the MetaDefender IT Access login page, framed in red in the screenshot below.

Support:

If you have followed the instructions above but are having difficulty **Logging Into The** **MetaDefender IT Access** **Console**, please open a [Support Case](https://my.opswat.com/portal/support) with the OPSWAT team via phone, online chat or form, or feel free to ask the community on our [OPSWAT Expert Forum](https://go2.opswat.com/s).
