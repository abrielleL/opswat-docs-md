# How do I change my PIN?

This article applies to the current MetaDefender IT Access Console, and all licensed MetaDefender IT Access accounts.

Pro Tip:

To confirm your changes when performing **Actions** or adjusting policy configurations and other settings for any of the devices and/or accounts that you manage, OPSWAT recommends that you use a security **PIN** as a verification measure.

This **PIN** can be set up via your **MetaDefender IT Access** **Console>User Profile>User Settings**, as illustrated in the screenshot below.

Once your security **PIN** has been set up, it can be changed, disabled, or reset in the event that it is forgotten.

## [How to change your PIN](/docs/macloud-sdp/kb/how-do-i-change-my-pin-#how-to-change-your-pin)

1. Log into your **MetaDefender IT Access Console** and click your account **Name** in the top, right-hand corner of the screen, to access your **User Profile**.
2. Under the **User Settings** tab, enter your **Current PIN** and **New PIN** in the fields provided.
3. Then, click **Update**, as illustrated below.

## [How to reset your PIN](/docs/macloud-sdp/kb/how-do-i-change-my-pin-#how-to-reset-your-pin)

Pro Tip:

Should you forget your security **PIN**, you are free to request a temporary PIN at any time, which will be generated and sent to you via email, for immediate use.

1. Log into your **MetaDefender IT Access Console** and click your account **Name** in the top, right-hand corner of the screen, to access your **User Profile**.
2. Under the **User Settings** tab, click **Forgot your PIN?**, as illustrated below.

3. In the pop-up window, confirm your decision to request a temporary PIN by clicking **Reset**, as illustrated below.

Support:

If you have followed the instructions above but are having difficulty **Changing Your Security PIN**, please open a [Support Case](https://my.opswat.com/portal/support) with the OPSWAT team via phone, online chat or form, or feel free to ask the community on our [OPSWAT Expert Forum](https://go2.opswat.com/s).
