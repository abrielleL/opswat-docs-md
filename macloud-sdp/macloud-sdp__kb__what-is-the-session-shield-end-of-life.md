# What is the Session Shield End of Life?

This article applies to all MetaDefender IT Access products deployed on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS systems.

No, the OPSWAT Session Shield is no longer available as a feature in MetaDefender IT Access or any other OPSWAT product - as of 9th June 2015.

The executable files for Session Shield have been removed, and the privacy proxy has been stopped.
