# What features of MetaDefender IT Access are available in on-premises deployment?

This article applies to the most updated releases deployed on Windows, macOS, iOS and Android systems.

The **MetaDefender IT Access** solution offers the flexibility of on-premises deployment utilizing My OPSWAT (v7.23). This article will provide you with comprehensive information on the available capabilities and supported platforms for on-premises deployment.
