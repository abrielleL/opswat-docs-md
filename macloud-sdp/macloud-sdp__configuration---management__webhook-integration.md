# Webhook Integration

Within MetaDefender IT Access, administrators are able to create webhooks and sort into groups based on triggered events. To setup your webhook integration, navigate to **Settings > Integrations > Webhook Integration**.

Create New Webhook

To create a new webhook, administrators can use the following steps:

1. Navigate to **Settings > Integrations > Webhook Integration**.
2. Select the **Webhooks** tab and click on **Create New** button on the right hand side.
3. Fill in the required fields for your webhook, enter your PIN, and select **Save**.

## [Create New Webhook Group](/docs/macloud-sdp/configuration---management/webhook-integration#create-new-webhook-group)

To create a new webhook group, administrators can use the following steps:

1. Navigate to **Settings > Integrations > Webhook Integration**.
2. Select the **Groups** tab and click on **Create New Group** button on the right hand side.
3. Fill in the required fields. *Note: You must assign as least one webhook to a group and at least one event to trigger the API call.*
4. Enter your PIN, and select **Save**.

## [Updating Webhooks](/docs/macloud-sdp/configuration---management/webhook-integration#updating-webhooks)

Administrators are able to activate/deactivate webhooks and update the webhook fields. To do so:

1. Navigate to **Settings > Integrations > Webhook Integration**.
2. Select the **Webhooks** tab.
3. To update webhook fields: click on the desired webhook. This will open a pop up window on the screen and allow you to update the fields. Confirm with your PIN, and select **Save**.
4. To activate/deactivate: toggle on/off the active switch. When a webhook is deactivated, it will be labeled as **Inactive**, and when active, it will be labeled as **Active**.

## [Updating Webhook Groups](/docs/macloud-sdp/configuration---management/webhook-integration#updating-webhook-groups)

Administrators can update their webhook group fields at any time. To do so:

1. Navigate to **Settings > Integrations > Webhook Integration**.
2. Select the **Groups** tab.
3. Click on the desired webhook and update the desired fields.
4. Confirm with your PIN, and select **Save**.

## [Deleting Webhooks and Webhook Groups](/docs/macloud-sdp/configuration---management/webhook-integration#deleting-webhooks-and-webhook-groups)

To delete any webhooks and webhook groups, simply click on the trash icon next to what you wish to remove. You will be required to enter your PIN to confirm the action.

*Note: If you attempt to remove a webhook that is assigned to a webhook group, you will receive an error in* *MetaDefender IT Access.* *You must unassign the webhooks you wish to remove before attempting to delete them.*
