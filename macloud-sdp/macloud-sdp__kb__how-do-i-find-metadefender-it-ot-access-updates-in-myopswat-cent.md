# How do I find MetaDefender IT-OT Access updates in MyOPSWAT Central Management emails?

This article is applied to MyOPSWAT Central Management, MetaDefender IT-OT Access releases deployed on Windows and Linux systems.

Info:

As of March 2024 MetaDefender IT Access was rebranded to OPSWAT Central Management. For more details, click here: <https://www.opswat.com/docs/macloud-sdp/release-notes/product-rebranding>

**Issue:** Update emails for MyOPSWAT Central Management contain references to many different OPSWAT products, which can be confusing for MetaDefender IT-OT Access (MD IT Access) customers.

**Resolution:** To identify updates relevant to MD IT Access, follow these steps:

- **Search the Email:** Look for the terms "Endpoint Security" or "Secure Access" within the update email.
- **Assess Relevance:** If either "Endpoint Security" or "Secure Access" appears prominently in the email, the update likely contains information relevant to MD IT Access.

- **No Relevant Updates:** If neither term is found, it is likely that there are no major updates to MD IT Access functionality in that particular email.
