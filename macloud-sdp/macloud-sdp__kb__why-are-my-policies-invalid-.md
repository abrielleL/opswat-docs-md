# Why are my policies invalid?

This article applies to all MetaDefender IT Access products deployed on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS systems.

As of 2 June 2020, MetaDefender IT Access customers require a **Platinum** tier account in order to enable or use **Threat Detection** via MetaDefender Cloud.

This means that free MetaDefender IT Access and free MetaDefender Cloud users can no longer enable or use Threat Detection via MetaDefender Cloud.

Please Note:

This update was made in alignment with OPSWAT’s current feature-based licensing tier system (for more information on our tier system, please [Read This](https://www.opswat.com/products/metaaccess#option_table) and [This](https://static.opswat.com/uploads/files/Product-Guide_compressed-1.pdf)).

If your account does not support threat detection via multi-scanning with MetaDefender Cloud, and you attempt to enable threat detection with MetaDefender Cloud as a scan source in one of your MetaDefender IT Access policies, that policy will be considered **Invalid**.

The system will notify you as to the nature and cause of your invalid policy, as illustrated below, and you can opt to either disable the notification or adjust the relevant setting. To fix invalid policies, please [Read This](/kb/how-can-i-fix-invalid-policies-on-my-account-).

Please Note:

In the event that you configure one or more invalid policies:

- You will not be permitted to confirm or implement any policy changes until the invalid setting has been corrected.
- MetaDefender Endpoint will not perform any threat detection scans, even if a scan is scheduled in your policy.
Pro Tip:

All users can still leverage MetaDefender’s ground-breaking **Threat Detection** technology by adding one or more native **MetaDefender Core** servers to their account, if they have not done so already.

To explore this option, or to explore qualifying **MetaDefender Cloud** options, please contact the [OPSWAT Sales](https://www.opswat.com/contact) team.
