# Can I monitor or manage mobile devices from MetaDefender IT Access?

This article applies to the current MetaDefender IT Access Console and all OPSWAT Mobile App releases deployed on Android and iOS devices.

Yes, **MetaDefender IT Access** offers intuitive, centralized monitoring and management of a range of device types, including:

- Desktops
- Laptops
- Servers
- Virtual Machines
- Tablets
- Smartphones

Additionality, **MetaDefender IT Access** supports a diverse range of popular operating systems, including:

- Windows
- macOS
- Linux
- Android
- iOS

To adjust policy aspects specific to mobile devices, please go to your **MetaDefender IT Access** **Console>Policy Management>Policies>Relevant Policy>Mobile Devices** tab, as illustrated below.

Pro Tip:

Your free MetaDefender IT Access account will allow for monitoring and management of an inventory of up to 50 devices across this full range, including mobile devices (tablets and smartphones).

For detailed instructions on how to Enroll Android and iOS devices to your MetaDefender IT Access account, please [Read This](/docs/macloud-sdp/kb/how-do-i-register-a-mobile-device-to-a-metaaccess-account-).
