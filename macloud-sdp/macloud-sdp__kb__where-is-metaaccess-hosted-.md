# Where is MetaDefender IT Access hosted?

This article applies to the current MetaDefender IT Access solution, as well as all component product releases deployed on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS systems.

MetaDefender IT Access is a global cloud product, designed to meet the cyber-security needs of diverse clients from around the world.

The physical location of the servers hosting a cloud product is instrumental due to the following factors:

- **Speed:** a cloud service located closer to users has faster response times.
- **Privacy:** due to certain privacy requirements, such as GDPR, organizations require that their data not leave the perimeter of their country or continent.

We value the performance experience and privacy of our users, and have selected our server locations based on their proximity to our users.

OPSWAT’s MetaDefender IT Access service is provided from servers in the following locations:

| **Tenant** | **Location** | **Console** | **API** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| US Tenant B | United States West Coast (Oregon) | [console.metaaccess-b.opswat.com](http://console.metaaccess-b.opswat.com) | [gears.opswat.com](http://gears.opswat.com) |
| US Tenant C | United States West Coast (Oregon) | [console.metaaccess-c.opswat.com](https://console.metaaccess-c.opswat.com) | [gears-beta.opswat.com](https://gears-beta.opswat.com) |
| EU Tenant | Northern Europe (Stockholm) | [console.metaaccess-eu.opswat.com](http://console.metaaccess-eu.opswat.com) | [gears-eu.opswat.com](http://gears-eu.opswat.com) |

Privacy Note:

All sensitive user and device data is automatically encrypted when stored.

Hosting In Other Locations:

Should your organization have unique security needs that require a host presence in a location other than those mentioned above, please contact [OPSWAT Sales](https://www.opswat.com/contact) to discuss the possibility of adding a new location to the list.
