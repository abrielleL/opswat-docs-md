# Assigning Webhooks and Webhook Groups

Administrators can assign webhook groups to *all* devices or to *select* devices. To learn more about creating webhooks and the webhook integration, please click [here](https://docs.opswat.com/macloud-sdp/configuration---management/webhook-integration).

## [Assign Webhooks to Notification Groups](/docs/macloud-sdp/configuration---management/assigning-webhook-groups#assign-webhooks-to-notification-groups)

To assign webhooks to notification groups, please follow the steps below:

1. Navigate to **Settings > Notification Groups.**
2. Select the notification group you wish to assign the webhook(s) to.
3. Select **+Assign Webhook** and a pop up window will appear with available webhooks to assign. Select the desired webhooks.
4. Once completed, select **Save** and confirm with your PIN.

## [Assign Webhook Groups to Device Groups](/docs/macloud-sdp/configuration---management/assigning-webhook-groups#assign-webhook-groups-to-device-groups)

To assign webhook groups to select device groups, please follow the steps below:

1. Navigate to **Inventory > Device Groups >** select your desired device group **> Webhooks**.
2. Toggle on **Webhooks** to activate the notifications.
3. Select **+Assign Webhook Group** and assign your desired webhook group.
4. Select **Save** and confirm with your PIN.

## [Assign Webhook Groups Globally](/docs/macloud-sdp/configuration---management/assigning-webhook-groups#assign-webhook-groups-globally)

To assign webhook groups globally, please follow the steps below:

1. Navigate to **Settings > Global > Notifications.**
2. Select **+Assign Group** and assign your desired webhook group.
3. Select **Save** and confirm with your PIN.

*Note: If you wish to remove any assigned webhook groups, simply select the trash icon in the row of the group you wish to delete. You will be required to confirm this action with your PIN.*
