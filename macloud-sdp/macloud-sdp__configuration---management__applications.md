# Applications

MetaDefender Endpoint detects applications installed on devices and reports back to MetaDefender IT Access.

From MetaDefender IT Access, administrators can view all applications installed on devices in their organization.

Administrators have a detailed view on a specific application such as what devices installed that application, vulnerabilities associated to it
