# Does OPSWAT Mobile App detect operating system vulnerabilities?

This article applies to all OPSWAT Mobile App releases deployed on Android and iOS devices.

Yes, the OPSWAT Mobile App detects operating system vulnerabilities and shares vulnerability data and warnings to both the MetaDefender IT Access Console (for administrators) and to the Mobile App (for end users).

To view Android/iOS vulnerability data, please log into your **OPSWAT Mobile App** and visit the **Securit**y and **Health** tabs.

Here you will find a range of vulnerability categories for your device, including but not limited to:

- **Password & Lockscreen**
- **Encryption**
- **OS Up to Date**
- **Ad Tracking**
- **Battery**
- **Hardware Performance**
