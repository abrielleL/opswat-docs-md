# How to Add Additional Domains for SSO Access in MetaDefender Cloud

 **Please note :** Domain changes may take up to 24–48 hours to reflect.

**Overview:**
This article outlines the steps required to add additional domains for Single Sign-On (SSO) access in the OPSWAT MetaDefender Cloud environment.

**Prerequisites:**

- Admin-level access to your OPSWAT account
- Existing SSO integration is configured

**Steps to Configure:**

1. **Identify the New Domain(s)**

   - Determine which domain(s) you want to enable for SSO use.
   - Make sure the domain(s) align with your organizational identity provider (IdP) configuration.
2. **Contact OPSWAT Support**

   - Submit a request via [OPSWAT Support Portal](https://my.opswat.com/portal/support-services)
   - Include:

     - Your OPSWAT account name
     - List of new domains to be added
     - Confirmation that these domains are part of your verified IdP setup
3. **MD Cloud Team Review & Action**

   - The OPSWAT support team will validate the domain request.
   - Once approved, they will associate the new domains with your account.

**Verify the Change:**

4. **Verify SSO Access**

   - After domain association, perform a test login using each new domain.
   - Confirm successful redirection to your identity provider and access to OPSWAT services.

Support:

If **Further Assistance** is required, please proceed to log a [support case or chatting with our support engineer](https://my.opswat.com/support).
