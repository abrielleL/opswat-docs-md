# What happens to my data when I use MetaDefender Cloud?

This article applies to all active MetaDefender Cloud license holders - whether your product is paid for or issued for evaluation purposes.

For more information on our range of licensing and evaluation options, please [Read This](https://metadefender.opswat.com/licensing).

MetaDefender Cloud utilizes state-of-the-art AWS infrastructure, set up throughout the United States and the European Union.

Our AWS framework ensures that requests from inside these regions get routed to geo-proximate instances, so that data never leaves the region from whence it was submitted.

Pro Tip:

Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure is designed for the highest levels of data security, boasting industry leading safeguards and security controls that achieve and exceed global privacy and compliance regulations.

To learn more, [Read This](https://aws.amazon.com/compliance/data-privacy/).

Please study the diagram below to learn more.

Pro Tip:

We are currently structuring plans to establish infrastructure in other nations, to comply with local privacy laws.

If you are not in the U.S. or E.U. and would like to use MetaDefender Cloud, but are unable to do so due to regulations that restrict data flow from your country, please contact the MetaDefender Cloud sales team [Here](https://www.opswat.com/contact).
