# Can MetaDefender Cloud prevent my application from being falsely identified as malicious?

This article applies to all active MetaDefender Cloud license holders - whether your product is paid for or issued for evaluation purposes. For more information on our range of licensing and evaluation options, please [Read This](https://metadefender.opswat.com/licensing).

One common use case for Multiscanning with MetaDefender Cloud is to scan the binaries of newly developed applications for potential false-positive detection by AV vendors before distribution to clients or the public.

Please Note:

OPSWAT will share potential false-positives or outbreaks with our AV vendors for investigation and analysis, unless otherwise requested from the API level. To explore our comprehensive API documentation, [Read This](https://docs.opswat.com/mdcloud/metadefender-cloud-api-v4/ref).
