### MetaDefender Cloud

***Cloud-native platform for file-borne threat prevention***

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/uykuzurltigm3k2v5p8v1ud0czow1fyr7in4rjeqvz21ogmx79fd8w5js5bgq1f1.png)

Using our REST API, organizations can easily add cloud-based detection and prevention of cybersecurity threats using deep content disarm and reconstruction (Deep CDR) and multi-scanning with 20+ anti-malware engines.

### Filescan.io

***Uncover Hidden Threats with Insightful Malware Analysis***

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/qe6l3pvs4p7x79x0w2l6zlq5tqbsoqg2kxdajg6bkg1jkprubm4bpz8yvdlsjhh9.png)

The free community version of **Filescan.io** is powered by **MetaDefender Sandbox** that operates 10 times faster than traditional sandboxes and uses 90% fewer resources. It uses adaptive threat analysis technology to detect evasive malware and extract relevant Indicators of Compromise (IOCs). Threat-agnostic scanning supports a wide variety of files and URLs to identify IOCs swiftly and efficiently.
