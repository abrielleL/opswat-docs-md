# Register an Account

> **Note**
>
> If you're a partner, please refer to [Partner Onboarding Guide](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/partner-guide/partner-onboarding-guide)

Register for a free My OPSWAT Portal account to sign in and explore OPSWAT solutions and products. With your account, you can also access all OPSWAT Cloud services and evaluate OPSWAT Cloud products at no cost before making any purchases.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/3lnfgyrxchwozyex45z80oz2ecthx96rjbn610b17uqdc9d9ivw2m1uaitztgujh.png)

**To create a new account in My OPSWAT Portal:**

1. Go to [My OPSWAT Portal](https://id.opswat.com/register)
2. Fill in all required fields
   1. First Name
   2. Last name
   3. Email: *This email will be used as username to sign in My OPSWAT Portal*
   4. Password: *Minimum password length is 13. The password must contain at least one lowercase letter, one uppercase letter, one number and one special character*
   5. Confirm Password
   6. Company Name
   7. Country
   8. How did you hear about OPSWAT? Check all that apply: *Select a referral source where you heard about OPSWAT. For some sources, OPSWAT may ask you to provide additional details.*
3. Select these checkboxes:
   1. Consent OPSWAT's [Terms of Service and Privacy Policy](https://www.opswat.com/legal)
   2. Optional: Subscribe to email communications from OPSWAT.
4. Follow the activation instructions in the email sent to your account (the email is valid for 24 hours).
