# My Information

> **Who can use this feature?**
>
> **User type:** Both [Personal](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/user-types-in-my-opswat-portal#personal-user) and [Organization](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/user-types-in-my-opswat-portal#organization-user) users

The **My Information** feature in the My OPSWAT Portal allows users to manage their personal details, preferences, security settings, and organizational affiliations.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/f6wmak1hqcou5rgx7qt5vxd57y73gyie3l208t1w43mhf9vhgpwyjbjpfzj5vrp5.png)

## Key Components

### 1. Personal Information

Update your basic details, such as name and contact information, to ensure accurate communication and identification within the Portal.​

[👉 ](https://docs.opswat.com/my/v2023.4.5/my-organization/my-information) [Learn more about Personal Information](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/my-information/personal-information)

### 2. Preferences

Customize communication preferences, manage email subscriptions, select topics of interest, and more.

[👉 ](https://docs.opswat.com/my/2024.2.3/my-organization/preferences)[Manage Your Preferences](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/my-information/preferences)

### 3. Security

Set up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), change your password, and enhance account security.

[👉 ](https://docs.opswat.com/my/2024.3.1/my-organization/security)[Configure Security Settings](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/my-information/my-security)

### 4. Organizations

View available organizations you're eligible to request joining.

👉 [See matching organizations](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/my-information/organizations#available-organizations)
