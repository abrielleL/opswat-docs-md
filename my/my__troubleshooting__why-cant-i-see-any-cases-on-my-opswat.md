# Why Can't I See Any Cases on My OPSWAT and Why Am I Getting the Message: "Your Data Is Being Initialized and Will Take Around 4-5 Hours"?

For **newly registered accounts**, the system may take approximately 4–5 hours to initialize your data. During this time, certain features on our Support Page might be temporarily unavailable. You might even see a message stating:

*"Your data is being initialized and will take around 4–5 hours. Please try signing in later to check if it's ready. Thank you for your patience."*

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/olakyogo557quhgvedkmq6a63ej5snl1z4nmtbhcwutru0m5r9sxacse77c9jkp1.png)

We appreciate your understanding and patience during this setup process.

If you need further assistance or if you’re unable to access your case, please [contact OPSWAT Support](https://my.opswat.com/support). Thank you!
