# Critical Alerts Users

> **Who can use this feature?**
>
> - **User type:** [Organization User](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/user-types-in-my-opswat-portal#organization-user)
> - **Role:** Full Access on  [Critical Alert Users](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/my-organization/single-organization#role-objects-and-permissions)

As an organization admin or a user with **Full Access** on  **Critical Alert Users** role object, you can add email addresses to a notification list called **Critical Alerts Users** by navigating to ***My Organization >  Critical Alerts Users***.

> **Note**
>
> You can add any email address to the Critical Alerts Users list, regardless of whether the individual has a My OPSWAT account.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/8d01ur5f1trvi2fn0jfk2svnnotbstaxb6y2mxdsbk7ihq08p6vgm4b6xeitauv3.png)

Individuals on this list receive important email alerts about:​

- **License Expirations**: Notifications are sent when **on-premises product licenses** are approaching their expiration dates, specifically at **60 days and 30 days** prior to expiration. If your organization has sub-organizations and the recipient has **View Only** or **Full Access** on the **License Management - Sub Organizations** role object, this notification covers the main organization and each sub-organization in one email. Addresses that do not belong to a My OPSWAT account always receive the single-organization version. See [Organization Licenses](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/license-management/my-organization-license#licenses-across-your-organization-hierarchy).​

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/w5i5ai841g7te8te4qgs4wya9f0rdq9i5q6czh5giwvrj58zpy1z2qm7dac54xfk.png)

- **Urgent Issues from OPSWAT**: Immediate updates about critical problems or important announcements from OPSWAT.

Members of your organization can subscribe or unsubscribe to critical alert notification under ***Support >*** [***Subscribe to Critical Alerts***](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/support-services/support-services#8-subscribe-to-critical-alerts)
