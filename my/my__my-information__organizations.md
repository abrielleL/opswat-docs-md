# Organizations

> **Who can use this feature?**
>
> **User type:** Both [Personal](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/user-types-in-my-opswat-portal#personal-user) and [Organization](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/user-types-in-my-opswat-portal#organization-user) users

The **Organizations** tab lets you view organizations with approved email domains that match yours and request to join them.

## Available Organizations

The **Available** **Organizations** section displays organizations with approved email domains that match yours. This helps you quickly identify and request access to the organization you belong to, streamlining your integration into My OPSWAT Portal.

> **Tip**
>
> Your email domain is the part of your email address that comes after the @ symbol and is usually shared by all members of a given organization. If your email address were [**abc@opswat.com**](mailto:abc@opswat.com), your email domain would be **opswat.com**.

### How Email Domains Work

When an organization’s administrator sets up an approved email domain that matches your email domain, you become eligible to request access to join that organization.

When a new eligible organization becomes available:

- A **notification banner** will appear at the top of your **Overview** page.
- Click **Request access** to go to the **Organizations** page, where you can submit your request.

### How to Request Access

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/t0o415vt6emgl2pr04lhvzrtv25z1odf39ixc5tfop34un7yvp2irzfbkptan51e.png)

To request to join an organization, take the following steps:

1. [Log in](https://my.opswat.com/) to My OPSWAT Portal.
2. Click your name in the upper-right corner and select **My Information**.
3. Navigate to **Organizations > Available Organizations**.
4. Click **Request to join** next to the organization you want to join
5. Fill out **Comment** and click **Submit Request**

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/1f6famdtut6j1jucfsvvuwm7poiqea0cfmi4pdr98rkujhnp2wrswp495253ihjl.png)

Once submitted:

- The request status will change to **Waiting for Approval**.
- Organization administrators will be notified.
- You’ll receive a system notification (   icon in the top-right corner) and an email once your request is approved or declined.

> **Please note**
>
> If your request is declined, you will not be able to resubmit unless invited again by the organization's admin.

### Can You Change Your Mind

Yes. You can cancel your request at any time before it’s approved or declined.

- Go to **My Information > Organizations**.
- Locate the request and click **Cancel request**.

### How to Remove an Organization from the List

The **Available Organizations** list shows all organizations with email domains that match yours. If there are organizations you don't wish to join—or if your request was declined and you'd like to clean up the list—you can remove them by clicking the  **trash bin** icon next to the organization name.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/h3t8tfkyap3nabsmmo00urf49hmtojkpsqsbtvvycbsrcr8rv3kjrzbqkn7qz709.png)

> **Info**
>
> Not available for **Waiting For Response** status

> **Warning**
>
> Once removed, the organization will no longer be suggested to you. You cannot request to join it again unless invited by an administrator.

## My Current Organizations

If you’re a member of multiple organizations, the **My Current Organizations** section displays a list of all organizations you belong to.

You can also select a **Default Organization** to streamline your login experience—this ensures you’re automatically directed to your preferred organization each time you sign in.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/k75vwsda3h04lzbf1g6eo49atnlei3objrq58r3fqvjj1ua2dg5yu5yjue7q0s9o.png)

#### If you are set to Inactive in an organization

An administrator can set your access to an organization to Inactive. Your account and activity history are preserved for audit, but your access to that organization is turned off.

- **During an active session:** If you are marked as Inactive (or removed) while signed in, your session ends right away, and you will see a message stating your access was revoked. Click **Back to sign in** to proceed. If you think this was a mistake, reach out to your organization's administrator.
- **In your organization list:** Organizations where you are Inactive are hidden from all places they usually appear, such as the organization menu, My Information > Organizations, and the Select Default Organization dialog. You can't view, switch to, or set them as your default.
- **If it was your default organization:** When you sign in next, you will need to select a new default if you belong to other active organizations. If there's only one active organization left, it automatically becomes your default. If you have none, you will continue as a personal (single) user.
