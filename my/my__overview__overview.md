## 1. Status Dashboard

The **Status Dashboard** provides a centralized view of key operational insights, directly from the **My OPSWAT Portal** homepage. It helps users quickly assess license health, stay on top of account- and system-level updates, and monitor learning progress—all in one place.

The visibility and level of detail in each section may vary based on your organization type and your account permissions.

### a. System & Account Notifications

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/kmzoh0faai7liq6myfr56uu3s2afn94gm7ftkzfor3lttgb7lb5z46pehjie8uii.png)

The **Notifications** section highlights important updates that may require your attention. These include:

- **Product lifecycle notices** (e.g., end-of-life or upgrade availability)
- **Password expiration reminders**
- **Prompts to enable multi-factor authentication (MFA)** for your organization
- **Organization access suggestions** (e.g., if relevant organizations are available for you to join)

Notifications appear at the top of your homepage and what you see may differ depending on your assigned permissions.

### b. License Health

The **License Health section** provides a snapshot of `license usage and health. Depending on your role and assigned permissions, this dashboard displays relevant license insights across your organization, sub-organizations, or customer organizations

**My Organization Licenses**

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/qu29os41fxchlj02mxmvq3ts3pk0zx9z6vmi3a5x4qur7crrhmlg4b1ei5s6vs4c.png)

When you belong to an organization that holds licenses and have either **View Only** or **Full Access permission** on **License Management**  (See [Roles](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/my-organization/single-organization#custom-roles) for details), the dashboard will display:

- **License Status:** A pie chart showing total cloud and on-premise product licenses associated with your organization, categorized into Active, Expiring and Expired.
- **Products with Near-Capacity Licenses:** Top three on-premise products with active licenses using more than 80% of their available slots.

> **Info**
>
> A product is considered near capacity if it has only 1 slot left (for ≤5 total slots) or if usage exceeds 80% (for > 5 slots).

Tip Click the  to view the full list of licenses in that category. You’ll be redirected to the **License Management > My Organization** page with the corresponding filter automatically applied.

If the following conditions are met, the **License Status Dashboard** will reflect license data across your organization and all sub-organizations:

- Your organization have sub-organizations
- **AND** You have **View Only** or **Full Access** on the **License Management**
- **AND** You have **View Only or Full Access** **permission** on the  **License Management - Sub Organizations**

**Customer Organization Licenses**

If the following conditions are met, you will see extended data on your License Status Dashboard.

- You belong to a **Partner organization** with assigned customers
- you have **View Only** or **Full Access** permission on the **License Management - Customer Organizations**

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/jiatw4jx12bafvi7qb2tmu4qs8v3vrln46xmq56lbm5z5sz6ecylmyoh10u3pbch.png)

In addition to your own organization license data, your dashboard includes **Customer Organization Licenses**

- **License status:** A pie chart summarizing license status (Active, Expiring Soon, Expired) across all managed customer organizations.
- **Organizations with Near-Capacity Licenses:** Top 3 customer organizations near full license usage.

Tip Click the  to view the full list of licenses in that category. You’ll be redirected to the **License Management > Customer Organizations** page with the corresponding filter automatically applied.

> **Info**
>
> A license is considered near capacity if it has only 1 slot left (for ≤5 total slots) or if usage exceeds 80% (for > 5 slots).

**No Organization or Licenses**

The License Status Dashboard will not appear on the homepage if

- You’re not in any organization
- **OR** Your organization has no license
- **OR** Your organization has valid licenses but you do not have permission on License Management

## 5. AI Content Recommendation

Beta This feature is in Beta. How recommendations are chosen is still being tuned.

The **AI content recommendations** suggests OPSWAT content based on the products your organization is licensed for and the content you have already engaged with, so you do not have to go looking for it.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/4uyj2otbee71y9e83qmxxx4qcx8hf0ic0avrwqpyfru3ivet7o5xhdea2tz39sue.png)

The banner is collapsed by default and shows the header **AI content recommendations** with the subtext *Personalized content based on your licenses and preferences*. Click **View Content** to expand it, and **Hide Content** to collapse it again.

When expanded, the banner shows a row of content cards covering a mix of content types. Each card shows the content type, the title, the date, and a button that opens the content. A **New** badge marks content published in the last 5 days that you have not opened yet.

Recommended content can include webinars, articles and knowledge base pages, product release notes, OPSWAT Academy courses, announcements, and resources from opswat.com.

Recommendations follow the organization you are currently working in, so switching organization changes what you see. They refresh when you reload the **Overview** page or return to it from another page, and a card you have opened is replaced by a different recommendation.

Each card has a thumbs up and a thumbs down control. Use them to tell OPSWAT whether the recommendation was useful to you. Your response is recorded.

> **AI Assistance must be turned on for organization users**
>
> This banner follows your organization's **AI Assistance** setting, the same setting that controls the AI Assistant chatbot. If your organization has AI Assistance turned off, the banner is hidden and no recommendations are generated. See [AI Assistance](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/my-organization/security#ai-assistance). A change to the setting applies from your next sign-in, or when you restart your session.
