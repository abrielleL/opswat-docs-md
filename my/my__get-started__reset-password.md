# Reset Password

You can reset your account password if

- You forget your original password.
- Your password is expired (As part of our security policy, passwords must be changed every 180 days) and an update is needed to secure your account.

## Forgot Password

1. On the [login](https://my.opswat.com/) page, enter your email
2. Click **Forgot password?** ![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/mnmxlw9i33u9gxnaymkrefqim12c1s0ryptk3bteoon9iztpyp0gyn7fj0whcb6u.png)
3. Check the email address, then click **Submit**![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/g0yscowxn4w2g8du8ii0yvct1art3mpv5zdbnajnzfek8sc2agmqpr7weyiwkw1u.png)
4. Open the password reset email and click **Reset password** button in the email.![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/44o9vhtc5iv0pnmd5hu4zsm6uzjnzuydm4y27p2h47lo4ssy3s5v1zw1oi05kp7j.png)
5. On the password reset page, enter your new password, then click **Reset Password** ![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/my4p19xdnhrt7lm1m35xpu0ax0zn5oxwwil9owubyijcq13gbixiscra5otb5jn4.png)
6. Your password reset process is now complete. You will then be redirected to the My OPSWAT Portal login page, proceed to log in to your account with your new password.  ![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/jqxmigj403yn9m7egj43vs6mjs3ek4gbwcljslvchs0h4trewxj6w5mecxaan4vk.png)

## Change Password

1. Log into [My OPSWAT Portal](my.opswat.com)
2. Go to your username in the top right corner and navigate to **My Information > Security** ![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/ad0dcvmlljkcc42tpraby9wvaoryaoax2e885020pjs9lha8fjxl1gwxtopqj7iv.png)
3. In the **Password** section, input your current password, your new password, and confirm the new password. Then click **Submit** ![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/842umedfkgwhcvap75uqfztmblfk0ilsfnx5gndtd04k4alivr3zbiu3tr3bf010.png)
