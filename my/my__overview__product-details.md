# Product Details

The **Product Details** page gives you a closer look at a specific OPSWAT product. Here you can find detailed information such as version data, documentation, and related news updates. To view a product’s details, click its entry from [Product Downloads](https://my.opswat.com/portal/product-downloads) or [License Management](https://my.opswat.com/portal/license-management/my-organization)

### Cloud-based Products

These are cloud services or platforms designed for integration into multi-cloud or hybrid cloud infrastructures. You’ll find their dedicated URLs under the **Product URL** section.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/krz59ss0s5q0yzc8zyxqyewz3twn07a9gy66rk62e32zgi9osvcd13msnmhsbt9a.png)

### On-premises products

Offered as hardware or software solutions that can be installed on-site, these products can be evaluated by clicking the **Download** button. This action opens the **Download** Dialog.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/kurujeouj10rr422795tnjwh4vnkwb6pi8zi2vjj77uodxm9pns46zvmmnf2ymk6.png)

This dialog displays all active and supported versions of products and provides information for customers to review and download.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/512ixbtpnpbrn9zf43x3ln8fmed21mqb1u9mwtbj89bwpj6fwnp3cl94ngml9obq.png)

For select products, such as ***MetaDefender Drive*** and  ***MetaDefender Managed File Transfer***, only the latest version is displayed to encourage upgrades.

For all other products, the dialog lists all active and supported versions, while automatically filtering out any versions that are considered end-of-line (i.e., when the next version’s release date exceeds 18 months).

**How to Download:**

1. Choose the desired platform and version from the list.
2. Click the **Download** button.
3. For advanced users, wget and cURL command options are also provided via dedicated buttons.

**Modules & Utilities:** Any additional modules or utilities available for the product are located in the **Modules & Utilities** section.

> **Note**
>
> My OPSWAT ensures you only see the currently supported versions, helping you maintain system efficiency and security.

>
