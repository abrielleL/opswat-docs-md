# Security Assurance

**Security Assurance** is your centralized hub to review OPSWAT’s security, privacy, and compliance posture. It is designed to help you quickly access trusted information, request compliance documentation, and complete security reviews with greater efficiency. Security Assurance is one of the two areas of the [Trust Center](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/trust-center/trust-center).

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/i5h46ygyisl4ktectvkkkzp94ei1f9ze2flnn2ddhme0zbdu9gisiezze9h81ylz.png)

**To access Security Assurance:**

1. Sign in to My OPSWAT Portal.
2. In the left menu, select **Trust Center**, then select **Security Assurance**.

**Security Assurance overview**

- Centralized hub for reviewing OPSWAT’s security, privacy, and compliance posture
- Access compliance documentation (e.g., SOC 2, ISO, certifications) after NDA acceptance
- Submit and manage security questionnaires in a self-service workflow
- Validate OPSWAT’s alignment with customer, regulatory, and security requirements

> **Third-Party Cookies Requirement**
>
> Security Assurance requires third-party cookies to be enabled in your browser. If third-party cookies are blocked, you will see a *"Third-Party Cookies Required"* error after clicking "Let's go" in the Welcome pop-up.
>
> ![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/b2o0imt400jxjqts9gb2bonh1od3ockynzbawefmia0inf4vprzryzvya2tuw0pz.png)
>
> To resolve this, enable third-party cookies in your browser's privacy/cookie settings and reload the page.
