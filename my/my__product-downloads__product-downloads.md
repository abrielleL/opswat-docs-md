# Product Downloads

> **Who can use this feature?**
>
> **User type:** Both [Personal](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/user-types-in-my-opswat-portal#personal-user) and [Organization](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/user-types-in-my-opswat-portal#organization-user) users

The Product Downloads page shows all available on-premises products that you can download.

**How to Access:** Select **Product Downloads** from the left navigation menu to open this page.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/t6yxgggtwrt7mgwek10cqks5r52okf9mci16shbd96veskyfo9upn8a9t8679gif.png)

**Displayed Information for Each Product:**

- **Product:** The name of the OPSWAT product.
- **Number of Downloads:** The total times the product files have been downloaded.
- **Last Download Time:** The date and time when the product was last downloaded.

Click on a product name to go to its [Product Details](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/overview/product-details) page. Click **Download** to open the Download OPSWAT Products dialog

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/9k9iykri0nqh8lj12d7hku17heea3n8457sd1tiu594habzhr7rhgeqlium3z2of.png)

> **Info**
>
> The **Time** filter is based on **Last download time.**

> **Please note:** MetaDefender Hardened Image is only displayed if the user belongs to an organization having active MetaDefender Kiosk Windows licenses
