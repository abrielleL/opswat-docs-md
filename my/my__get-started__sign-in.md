# Sign in

## To log in to your account:

1. Go to [My OPSWAT Portal](https://my.opswat.com/) and enter your email address.
2. Click Sign in
3. Start using our features.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/htqc8zwgxn0ggzg5vixfmwh6z2qeqbrfzmij2icgp75wdgp8mwvgbanj0y2fp001.png)

### SSO Behavior (When Enabled)

When SSO is enabled for your organization, the login flow works as follows:

1. Go to [My OPSWAT Portal](https://my.opswat.com/) and enter your email address.
2. If SSO is enabled, you are automatically redirected to your configured Identity Provider (e.g., Microsoft Entra or Okta) for authentication.
3. Upon successful authentication, the user is redirected back to My OPSWAT Portal.

## To reset your password:

1. Click **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter the account's email address and select **Submit**

Further reading at [Reset Password](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/reset-password)

Note If you do not receive the password reset email, please follow the instructions in [this document](https://docs.opswat.com/my/troubleshooting/didnt-receive-a-reset-password-email) to resolve the issue.

## To go back to an expired session:

When your My OPSWAT Portal session ends, the **Session Timeout** message shows.

To go back to your session, click **Sign in** and enter your password.
