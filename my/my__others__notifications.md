# Notifications

> **Who can use this feature?**
>
> **User type:** Both [Personal](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/user-types-in-my-opswat-portal#personal-user) and [Organization](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/user-types-in-my-opswat-portal#organization-user) users

The **Notifications** feature keeps you informed about important system and account updates.

Notifications are grouped into two types:

### Direct Notifications

These appear when there are changes related to your organization or user account:

- You’re invited to or removed from an organization.
- You receive a new user join request to your organization
- Your request to join an organization is approved or declined
- You send, receive, approve, or decline a request to join an organization.
