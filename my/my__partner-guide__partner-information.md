# Partner Information

> **Who can use this feature**
>
> - **User type:** [Organization user](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/user-types-in-my-opswat-portal#organization-user)
> - **Organization type:** Partner Organization
> - **Role:** View Only/Full Access on [Organization General Information](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/my-organization/single-organization#role-objects-and-permissions)

The **Partner Information** tab provides OPSWAT partners with key details about their partnership profile.

**How to access:**

1. Go to **My Organization** from the left navigation menu, then select **Partner Information**.
2. From the **Partner Portal > Overview** page, click the **View Partner Details** hyperlink.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/sdxxjxclv3omkbmjs22ndb15y300xhn5b3lgei753g72kt6xjvj3im3kv2zhg01n.png)

The **Partner Information** tab shows the following details related to your partnership with OPSWAT:

- Account Type
- Partner Program Level
- Sales Region
- Partner Manager
- Solution Focus
- Industry Focus

> **Note**
>
> If any of your partner information is incorrect or incomplete, contact your OPSWAT Partner Manager or contact us via [OPSWAT Support](https://my.opswat.com/portal/support).
