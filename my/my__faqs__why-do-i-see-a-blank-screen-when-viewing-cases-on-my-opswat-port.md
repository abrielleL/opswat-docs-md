# Why Do I See a Blank Screen When Viewing Cases on My OPSWAT Portal?

If you’re experiencing a **blank screen** (as shown in the screenshot below) when viewing your submitted cases on [my.opswat.com](https://my.opswat.com/), the issue is most likely related to **cookie settings**.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/malpzun1hau8454098soa107htyzx0pik37aopdguu2ecaw22p0ro65o73y7qz6b.png)

This occurs when the **cookie policy has not been accepted**. Due to recent changes in OPSWAT’s cookie policy, users must **explicitly allow cookies** for the portal to display content correctly.

### Resolution Steps

#### **Scenario 1: Using the “Paint Tray” Icon**

1. Locate the paint tray icon at the bottom left of your page.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/iqiq216i772h6bn6rw2cm06sjcd0xox6j63xrt9txulyhlqmd1aw0xp0nfg9ayvv.png)

2. Click the icon, then select “Allow All.”

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/u389t6nixl3nmctzoni10c8sf5185t0deho320nrn20109u6zrnab6uumilys90j.png)

3. Your case details should now be visible.

#### **Scenario 2: Adjusting Cookie Permissions Manually**

**Step 1 – Check Cookie Settings**

1. Open [my.opswat.com](https://my.opswat.com/).
2. Click the **lock or shield icon** next to your browser’s address bar.
3. Ensure **third-party cookies** are **allowed** for this site.
   - If already allowed, proceed to Step 2.

**Step 2 – Clear Existing Cookies**

1. Go to your browser’s settings.
2. Clear all cookies and cached data.
3. Revisit [my.opswat.com](https://my.opswat.com/).
4. A banner will appear prompting you to accept cookies, click **“Accept All.”**

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/qucfveb4ogj0wszok61q1tvcqlycc7ztph0mzefws4v3ctnbod67lhyt5llkt0bv.png)

5. Your case details should now be visible.

After accepting the cookie policy, your case details should load normally, and the blank screen issue will be resolved.
