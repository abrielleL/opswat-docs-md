# Trust Center

The **Trust Center** in My OPSWAT Portal is where you verify OPSWAT’s security posture and work with us on product security. It brings together security, privacy, and compliance information, security questionnaires, and vulnerability reporting in one place.

**To access the Trust Center**

1. Sign in to My OPSWAT Portal.
2. In the left menu, select **Trust Center** to expand it.
3. Select the area you want to open.

**Trust Center areas**

- [**Security Assurance**](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/trust-center/security-assurance)**:** Review OPSWAT’s security, privacy, and compliance posture. Access compliance documentation such as SOC 2, ISO, and other certifications after NDA acceptance, and submit and manage security questionnaires
- [**Vulnerability Disclosure**](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/trust-center/vulnerability-disclosure)**:** Report a security vulnerability you found in an OPSWAT product or service and follow it through to resolution.
