# How to Export Scan Results for Submission

Scan results are mandatory for submitting a false detection. They provide essential context, help the Threat Detection team identify the source of a false positive, and capture details users may miss when filling out the form.

- **MetaDefender Core**: Click the drop-down next to **Export result** and select **JSON export**. The JSON file includes details from multiscanning, reputation, and sandbox.![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/1h561fbtm2i26qo5jck54ke7jn2eidmht9f26dpxx9vtb4oaxjccestx1oseqicw.png)
- **Standalone Sandbox**: Click **Download Report** and select **As Raw JSON**.![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/igo8jkv3iud17rj894xuavhihtuvlw40yanvk6l50ifz9x6gpzmruttowrmxv3ge.png)

> **Warning**
>
> The JSON format is required, as it enables automation during the initial submission triage.
