# Where do I find my license keys?

The My OPSWAT Portal provides a  feature for managing your licensed products. By navigating to the [**License Management**](https://my.opswat.com/license-management/my-organization) page, you can view all the product licenses that your organization owns. These licenses are categorized into 2 sections: **Cloud Services Licenses** and **On-Premises Product Licenses.** For more detailed information about this feature, please refer to our documentation [Feature Overview](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/license-management/license-management)

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/ttpoej4420jrk0idn1wx9p2qz5bwpmm1m3hla8s5k00pu4otgdu48qqwnieii8w5.png)

If you’re unable to locate your licenses, it’s recommended to reach out to your organization’s administrators. You can find their contact information on the **My Organization** page under the ‘Users’ tab.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/vkqw6tia3hpvurxlzfv82hs8731y7nfmbws48os864nx62u5is813bsgbb2eag4p.)

If you require further assistance or your organization's administrator is unable to resolve this, please contact [OPSWAT Technical Support](https://my.opswat.com/support) by navigating to **Support** page for assistance.
