# Feature Overview

> **Who can use this feature?**
>
> **User type:** Both [Personal](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/user-types-in-my-opswat-portal#personal-user) and [Organization](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/user-types-in-my-opswat-portal#organization-user) users

The **License Management** feature in the My OPSWAT Portal provides a centralized interface to view and manage OPSWAT product licenses, tailored to your organizational role and permissions.

## Key Components

### 1. Organization Licenses

View and manage all product licenses associated with your current organization.

👉  [Organization Licenses](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/license-management/my-organization-license)

### 2. Organization and its sub-organizations' Licenses

If you have the necessary permissions, access licenses for your organization and its sub-organizations.

👉  [Organization and its sub-organizations' Licenses](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/license-management/my-main-sub-organizations-licenses)

### 3. Organization Hardware

Gain visibility into your organization’s OPSWAT hardware and easily track shipping information for each order right.

👉  [Organization Hardware](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/hardware-orders/organization-hardware)

### 4. For Partners Customer Licenses

Partners, such as Distributors and Resellers, can view and manage licenses of their customer organizations.

👉 [Customer License Management](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/partner-guide/customer-licenses)

### 5. For Partners Customer Contracts

Partners, such as Distributors and Resellers, can view all opportunity contract details associated with each customers.

👉 [Customer Contract Management](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/partner-guide/customer-contracts)
