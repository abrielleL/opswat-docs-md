# Users and Roles

Manage user access and permissions within your organization using **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** in My OPSWAT Portal.

In My OPSWAT Portal, the **Users** and **Roles** features operate differently depending on your organization's configuration.

### Single Organization

- Default setup for all organizations.
- Straightforward user and role management within a single entity.
- Ideal for organizations without the need to segment access or resources.​

👉 [Learn more about managing users and roles in a Single Organization](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/my-organization/single-organization)

### Organization with Sub-organizations

- Enables extended management to cover multiple levels by creating sub-organizations under a main organization.
- Allows for separate management of licenses, scan limits, users, and roles.
- Requires enabling the "Manage Sub-organizations" setting by contacting OPSWAT Support or your Customer Success Manager.​

👉 [Learn more about managing users and roles in Organizations with Sub-organizations](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/my-organization/multi-level-organizations)

### Partner Organizations

- Partner organizations with extra built-in roles and additional partner-specific role objects.

👉 [Learn more about managing roles in Partner Organizations](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/partner-guide/partner-role-management)
