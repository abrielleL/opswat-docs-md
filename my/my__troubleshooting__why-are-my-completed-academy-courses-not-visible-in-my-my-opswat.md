# Why are my completed Academy courses not visible in my My OPSWAT profile?

If you have completed OPSWAT Academy courses but your **Completed Courses** section appears blank, please verify the following:

### Preliminary Checks

- **Completion Status:** Ensure the courses were fully completed and you received a passing grade.
- **Account Alignment:** Confirm you are using the same email address for both the **OPSWAT Academy** and the **My OPSWAT Portal**.
- **Expiration Date:** Certificates earned several years ago (e.g., 2022–2023) may have expired. **Expired certificates are automatically removed** from the My OPSWAT Portal display.

---

### Resolution

While expired certificates are hidden from your My OPSWAT profile, your full academic record remains accessible via the Academy's dedicated portal.

#### Steps to Access Your Certificates

1. Sign in to [My OPSWAT](https://my.opswat.com/) using your OPSWAT ID.
2. Go directly to the [OPSWAT Academy Certifications page](https://learn.opswatacademy.com/certifications), all earned credentials, including those that have expired, will be listed here.

---

### Additional Information

- To obtain an active certificate, you may retake the relevant courses at any time.
- Once you successfully complete a course again, the renewed certification will automatically sync and reappear in your **My OPSWAT**.
