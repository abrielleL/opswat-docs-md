# Why don't I see the MetaDefender Kiosk Hardened Image under MetaDefender Kiosk on the Product Downloads page?

### **Issue**

You have a MetaDefender Kiosk K-Series license, but the Hardened Image download does not appear under Product Downloads in your My OPSWAT Portal account.

### **Explanation**

The MetaDefender Kiosk Hardened Image shows up in My OPSWAT Portal only if:

1. MetaDefender Kiosk license is associated with your Organization
2. Your current account is part of the your Organization in My OPSWAT

### Solution

Check if you’re added to the correct organization in My OPSWAT Portal. If not, contact your administrator to request access.

**1. Steps** **for the Organization's Administrator to add a user to the correct organization so that they can see the Harden Image download option:**

1. The Super Admin should log in to [https://my.opswat.com](https://my.opswat.com/)
2. Ensure you are in the correct Organization (main Organization)
3. From the left menu go to My Organization  > Users and invite the user you would like to have added to this organization.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/jkahekddcgo2zy47hglz6svfhdhjl1wqi015jfbw0xmxas28hyuvvoa67t36ihgv.png)

Besides, you can also find the list of potential organizations you might belong to and request to join.

**2. Steps for request to join available associated organizations**

1. [Log in](https://my.opswat.com/) to My OPSWAT Portal.
2. Click your name in the upper-right corner and select **My Information**.
3. Navigate to **Organizations > Available Organizations**.
4. Click **Request to join** next to the organization you want to join
5. Fill out **Comment** and click **Submit Request**

👉 **Further reading at **[**Available Organizations**](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/my-information/organizations#available-organizations)

**Note**: In case of a situation where your organization does not have a Super Admin, please reach out to [support@opswat.com](mailto:support@opswat.com) and we will help you to add your account to be a Super Admin.
