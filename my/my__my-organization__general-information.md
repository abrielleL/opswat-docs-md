# General Information

> **Who can use this feature?**
>
> - **User type:** [Organization user](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/user-types-in-my-opswat-portal#organization-user)
> - **Role:** View Only/ Full Access on [Organization General Information](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/my-organization/single-organization#role-objects-and-permissions)

As an organization admin or user with **Full Access** on **Organization General Information**, you can view and update your organization's basic details.​

**To access this tab:**

1. Go to **My Organization** in the left navigation menu.
2. Select **General Information**.​

**To edit information:**

- Click **Edit** to update your organization's details.
