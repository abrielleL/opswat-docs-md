# How to change the email address for My OPSWAT account?

To update the email address associated with your My OPSWAT account, follow the steps outlined below:

**Step 1: Submit a Data Subject Request Form**

To change your email address, you must complete the [Data Subject Request form](https://www.opswat.com/legal/data-subject-request-form). This form is used to verify your request and ensure the security of your account.

**Step 2: Receive the Confirmation Email**

After submitting the form, a confirmation email will be sent to the email address you provided.

**Step 3: Confirm Your Submission**

To complete the email update process, you must click the confirmation link in the email within **five days** of submitting the request. Failure to do so will require you to submit a new request.

If you encounter any issues or do not receive the confirmation email, please check your spam/junk folder or contact [OPSWAT Support](https://my.opswat.com/support) for assistance.
