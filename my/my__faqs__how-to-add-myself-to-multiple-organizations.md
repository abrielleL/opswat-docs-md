# How to add myself to multiple organizations?

It is possible to add yourself to multiple organizations; however, this task cannot be completed independently. To be added to multiple organizations, please follow these steps:

1. **Contact Your Organization’s Administrator**
   - Reach out to your administrator and request access to additional organizations. They have the necessary permissions to add you.
2. **Reach Out to OPSWAT Technical Support**
   - If you require further assistance or your administrator is unable to add you, please contact [OPSWAT Technical Support](https://my.opswat.com/support) for assistance.

Providing relevant details, such as your account email and the names of the organizations you need access to, will help speed up the process.
