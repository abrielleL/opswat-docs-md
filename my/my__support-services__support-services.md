# Support

> **Who can use this feature?**
>
> **User type:** Both [Personal](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/user-types-in-my-opswat-portal#personal-user) and [Organization](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/user-types-in-my-opswat-portal#organization-user) users

The **Support Page** is where users can efficiently find solutions to their issues. Whether through interacting with the [AI Chatbot (Ozzy)](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/support-services/support-services#2-chat-with-ai-assistant), [submitting a case](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/support-services/support-services#4-submit-a-support-case), or [manage cases](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/support-services/support-services#5-manage-your-cases), this feature of My OPSWAT Portal is designed to streamline your support experience.

This guide walks you step by step through every feature so you can resolve issues and keep your operations running smoothly.

To access this page, click [Support](https://my.opswat.com/support) item on the left menu

Notes *Information displayed on the Support Page may vary slightly depending on each user's organizational level and support tier, and* [*AI Assistance*](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/my-organization/security#ai-assistance)* setting.*

## 1. Chat with AI Assistant (Ozzy)

### AI Assistance is Enabled

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/rmv3zklsfbzemi4uokc51rgd5p6vwesiylt8hxydrlq6fka098sj3uj29o97wa5e.png)

When [AI Assistance](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/my-organization/security#ai-assistance) **is enabled**, you can either click the **Start Chat** button in the Ozzy AI Assistant banner or **Ask Ozzy** button in the top-right corner. The chatbot dialog expands, and you can continue the conversation to find what you need.

The AI assistant can help with:

- Solutions to common issues.
- Links to relevant resources, such as documentation or FAQs.
- The option to escalate and submit a case.

> **Pro Tip**
>
> Be specific about your product and describe any error messages

### AI Assistance Is Disabled

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/retzjui7vyu2fjigotdd0q99m0zl2cueuuor1l7blbhmg9zeaekmymfz9h04yqw7.png)

If [AI Assistance](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/my-organization/security#ai-assistance) is disabled, the Ozzy AI banner is hidden on the Support landing page, and the chatbot button in the top-right corner changes to **Chat Support**. Any new chat connects you directly to a support agent without AI triage.

> **Info**
>
> It may take longer to process requests or receive a response with support agent. AI assistance helps answer questions faster and resolve common issues automatically.

## 2. Human Chat Assistance

You can also connect with an OPSWAT support agent in real time, with a case automatically created in the ticketing system.

- Open the chatbot via the Ozzy AI Assistant banner or the chatbot button in the top-right corner.
- Enter "**Transfer to Agent**" or "**I would like to speak with an agent**".
- A support case is created as a record of the interaction for proper follow-up.
- End the chat by typing "end chat" and clicking yes on the confirmation dialog.

> **Info**
>
> If **AI Assistance** is disabled, any new chat connects you directly with a support agent, so you won’t need to request a transfer. ![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/74lnmon3awtjr20i7j1p90wyrdy6codxo5xzhqfty1szmuqgy21e3c76tonfta7h.png).

## 7. Manage Your Cases

The **Support Cases list** provides an organized view of: **My Cases**, **Organization Cases**, **Partner Cases**, **Customer Cases,** and **False Detection Submission .**

Your available tabs depend on your account type and permissions:

| Tab | Who can see | What It Shows |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| My Cases | All users | Cases you personally submitted |
| Organization Cases | Organization members with permissions | Cases submitted by other users from your organization |
| Partner Cases | Customer organization members with permissions | Cases submitted by partners on behalf of your organization |
| Customer Cases | Partners with permissions | Cases submitted for customers, by yourself and other users from your organization |
| False Detection Submissions | All users | Auto-created cases from false detection reports |
| RMA cases | All users | Auto-created cases from RMA reports |
| All cases | All users | A unified view of every case you have access to |
