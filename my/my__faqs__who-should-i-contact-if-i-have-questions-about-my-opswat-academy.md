# Who Should I Contact If I Have Questions About My OPSWAT Academy Courses?

If you need help with your OPSWAT Academy courses, like accessing courses or downloading certificates, our support team is ready to assist you. You can open an OPSWAT Academy support case by following these steps:

1. Click **Need help?** link on the Academy Overview tab, which opens the form with the product already set to OPSWAT Academy.

   ![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/o0w6cm8foxgcsqln6ky4kmzqy8bjgv0abdqlxc3js3moi7ux2zkhtssbcv30av49.png)

1. Choose an Academy issue type

   ![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/JYvy/91a7m5yl49oq6kngbowle92icgbnxk8wojbhzm4jp9ar81cn740lzuma8uf9givn.png)
2. Complete the remaining fields following [Case Submission Guide](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/support-services/submit-a-case)
