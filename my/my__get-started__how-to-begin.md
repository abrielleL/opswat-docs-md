# Getting started

Use this guide to begin your journey with **My OPSWAT Portal**. Whether you're a new customer, partner, Academy student, or prospective user, this page outlines what you need to access the Portal and make the most of its core features.

## Access Requirements

To access My OPSWAT Portal, you need one of the following:

- A registered **OPSWAT account.**
- A valid **invitation to join an organization** or Academy
- Access via your organization 's Identity Provider such as Microsoft Entra or Okta.

> **NOTE**
>
> You don’t need an active license to access the portal. Feature availability depends on your user role and assigned permissions.

## Recommended Screen Sizes & Browsers

### Screen Sizes Support

My OPSWAT Portal is only optimized for screen size of **1400px or higher**. Devices with smaller screens, such as tablets and mobile phones, might not display the UI properly.

> ## First-Time Setup Steps

Follow these steps to get started:

### Register a My OPSWAT Portal Account

If you don't have an OPSWAT account:

1. Visit [My OPSWAT Portal](https://my.opswat.com/)
2. Click **Register**
3. Complete the registration form
4. Verify your email address

Further reading at [Register an Account](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/register)

### Sign In to My OPSWAT Portal

1. Go to [My OPSWAT Portal](https://my.opswat.com/)
2. Enter your email address
3. Click **Sign In**

Further reading at [Sign in](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/sign-in)

My OPSWAT Portal will set up your account type as follows:

- **Personal Account:** Created by default when you register on your own
- **Organization Account:** Your account becomes an organization account when you are invited to join an organization.

For more information about account types, see [User Types in My OPSWAT Portal](https://www.opswat.com/docs/my/get-started/user-types-in-my-opswat-portal)
