# Are Microsoft Visio Stencils Available for NetWall Equipment?

This article applies to all MetaDefender NetWall products and is relevant for all supported releases.

At this time, OPSWAT does **not** provide official Microsoft Visio stencils specifically for NetWall equipment.

**Alternative Options:**

- Customers can use standard or generic network and security device shapes in Visio to represent NetWall appliances in their diagrams.
- This approach allows visual documentation of networks, while maintaining clarity in the design, even without dedicated stencils.
