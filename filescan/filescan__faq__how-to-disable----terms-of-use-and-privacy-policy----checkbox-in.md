# How to disable “Terms of Use and Privacy Policy” checkbox in MetaDefender Aether?

When processing a file, users will be prompted to agree to **Terms of Use** and **Privacy Policy**

This is an optional feature that, if it is not needed, can be disabled by following the steps below:

1. In the top right corner click the User Icon and select Admin Panel
2. Select Settings at the top of the page and navigate to Configuration > User Management > Terms of Use
3. Disable the ASK_TERMS_CONSENT checkbox and click Save to save the settings:
