# Registration

### [Step 1- Sign-up](/docs/filescan/3.1.1/adminguide/e-mail-notifications#step-1--sign-up)

You need to click on "**Sign in**" first in the right corner of the page in order to choose the "**Sign up now**" function.

Now you may click on the "**Sign up now**"

### [Step 2 - Register](/docs/filescan/3.1.1/adminguide/e-mail-notifications#step-2---register)

Start your registration under the "**Sign-up"** page and insert your email and desired password for future logins.

Info

Required Agreement to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are required.

### [Step 3 - Confirm your email](/docs/filescan/3.1.1/adminguide/e-mail-notifications#step-3---confirm-your-email)

Afterwards, you will receive an email to your email address with a confirmation link to verify your account.

Please click on the "**Confirm your email**" link

It will direct you back to the **Homepage** of MetaDefender Aether

See the "Technical Datasheet" for a complete list of features: <https://docs.opswat.com/filescan/datasheet/technical-datasheet>
