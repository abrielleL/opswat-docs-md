# Is MetaDefender Sandbox being discontinued with MetaDefender Aether launch?

View as Markdown Ask ChatGPT about this page Ask Claude about this page

**MetaDefender Aether Overview**

On January 26th 2026, we introduced **MetaDefender Aether**, an evolution of our existing MetaDefender Sandbox 3.0.0, enhanced with additional capabilities to provide broader and more integrated threat defence.

**MetaDefender Aether** brings together **MetaDefender Sandbox, Threat Intelligence, and Threat Hunting** into a unified solution designed to deliver deeper insights and stronger protection across the threat lifecycle.

---

**Product Availability and Packaging**

**Continued availability of MetaDefender Sandbox**

**MetaDefender Sandbox** remains a fully supported product and is not being discontinued or designated as End of Life. Customers can continue to rely on Sandbox as a core component of their security stack with ongoing support and development.

**Standalone Sandbox Option**

For organisations that require sandboxing capabilities only, **MetaDefender Sandbox** continues to be available for purchase as a standalone solution. This option provides core sandbox functionality without the additional Threat Intelligence components included in **MetaDefender Aether.**

**Commercial Packaging of MetaDefender Aether**

**MetaDefender Aether** is a premium offering that combines the capabilities of Sandbox and Threat Intelligence into a single, comprehensive solution. As an advanced, integrated product, it is available as a paid offering reflecting the expanded functionality and value it delivers.

**Single download with license-based functionality**

The **MetaDefender Aether** package available for download from the MYOPSWAT Portal is delivered as a single installer. The functionality and product experience are determined by the applied license key — customers with a **MetaDefender Aether** license will see the platform operate as **MetaDefender Aether**, while customers with a **MetaDefender Sandbox** license will continue to experience the product as **MetaDefender Sandbox**. This approach simplifies deployment while ensuring customers receive the capabilities aligned with their licensed entitlements.

See the "Technical Datasheet" for a complete list of features: <https://docs.opswat.com/filescan/datasheet/technical-datasheet>
