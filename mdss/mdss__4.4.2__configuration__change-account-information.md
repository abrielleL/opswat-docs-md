# Change your account information

From the top bar, click on the **User** icon and navigate to **Profile Information**.

### [Change password](/docs/mdss/4.4.2/configuration/change-account-information#change-password)

1. Please enter the current password associated with the account.
2. After validating it, you will be allowed to provide the new password and to confirm it.

3. To finish changing the password, please click on **Update password**.

### [Change email](/docs/mdss/4.4.2/configuration/change-account-information#change-email)

1. Please enter the current password associated with the account.
2. After validating it, you will be allowed to provide a new email address.
3. To finish changing the email, please click on **Update email**.

### [Change full name](/docs/mdss/4.4.2/configuration/change-account-information#change-full-name)

1. Provide the new full name you wish to use for the account.
2. To finish changing the full name, please click on **Update full name**.
