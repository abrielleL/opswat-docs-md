# Enrolling the product to Central Management

To enroll MetaDefender Storage Security into My OPSWAT Central Management, these steps should be followed:

1. In the top-right corner, next to the username, click the **OPSWAT Central Management** button. The button keeps this label after enrollment; only its icon changes to show that the product is connected. Users without permission to change the enrollment see the label but cannot click it.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/k8zL/grp4n76t30xbwwmh1cqd0oi4sfh64mt1wwo8dbiirrjj8bb3upyvdgrpcad2hkqv.png)

2. Clicking the button will open a form containing the necessary fields to enroll. These are **Server URL**, which is the address of the Central Management server to which you want to enroll the product, and **Registration Code**, which can be found in the Central Management UI by navigating to **Settings -> Global**. The registration code will be present on that page.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/k8zL/pxxzbu21ya0b9e373re6efg1v5jdqttp1x26la2l4t3m97pjug7rotmwda2qvsnh.png)

3. After completing the form, click **Enroll now**. If the information is correct, MDSS should display a message saying that enrollment was successful.

Once you have successfully enrolled your MetaDefender Storage Security instance, the button **OPSWAT Central Management** will reveal **Product Management** information about the connection to the **OCM** platform.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/k8zL/lvdgt608znjsppv40l8nrmy50v4ai8dvh4uk21j9bdpteip79pieoo2j9ye5cfnf.png)

Additionally, navigating to the Central Management server, you should be able to see your enrolled instances under the **Inventory > Services** tab

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/k8zL/7o0sjrextl05stcctu6kf68z2tbm7pjgaerwkip4g7nuq5sesldgvqwhc9573yb8.png)

Furthermore, clicking on the card above will take you to a page where more information about each instance can be seen:

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/k8zL/ivfnh9wqcl0tynlim868ubyp3gkg6790r7h5h5rzptlugjmp51ffo19m801zjdgn.png)
