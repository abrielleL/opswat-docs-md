# MetaDefender InSights Intelligence Feeds

There is no one-size-fits-all approach to utilizing intelligence data. Each organization faces unique challenges, and teams require solutions that satisfy different use cases and work with different capabilities to operationalize and leverage this data. Our intelligence products are distributed in curated feeds of technical indicators that are shaped towards specific use cases, each intended to give defenders advantages in key areas of the threat landscape.

In the following pages, we provide an overview of our MetaDefender InSights intelligence feeds.
