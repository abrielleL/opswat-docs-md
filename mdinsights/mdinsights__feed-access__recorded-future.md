# Recorded Future Intelligence Portal

OPSWAT's InQuest Labs threat intelligence data is currently available as a direct integration for Recorded Future Intelligence customers through Intelligence Cards and in the Recorded Future Intelligence Portal.

Accessible thanks to OPSWAT's close partnership with Recorded Future, existing Recorded Future customers will find automatic inclusion of OPSWAT InQuest Labs threat intelligence convenient as they investigate observables in the analyst view. InSights indicators can provide contextualization and enrichment of data during platform searches and can help add additional insight and verdicts to observables.
