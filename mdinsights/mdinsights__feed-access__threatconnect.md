# ThreatConnect Marketplace

### [ThreatConnect Marketplace](/docs/mdinsights/feed-access/threatconnect#threatconnect-marketplace)

MetaDefender InSights is currently available as a featured integration for ThreatConnect Platform customers through the TC Exchange:

[**https://threatconnect.com/marketplace/inquest/**](https://threatconnect.com/marketplace/inquest/)

Current ThreatConnect Platform customers can automatically ingest MetaDefender InSights feed data directly in their threat intelligence platform view. InSights indicators can provide contextualization and enrichment of indicators in the platform search and detail views, and ThreatConnect Platform adds additional value to the intelligence by surfacing additional assessment factors, contextual scoring, and data enrichment.
