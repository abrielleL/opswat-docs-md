# MetaDefender InSights

## [Welcome to MetaDefender InSights!](/docs/mdinsights/metadefender-insights/metadefender-insights#welcome-to-metadefender-insights)

MetaDefender InSights is OPSWAT's suite of threat intelligence feeds designed to help customers maintain a robust, threat-informed defense against cybersecurity threats.

## [Documentation](/docs/mdinsights/metadefender-insights/metadefender-insights#documentation)

In this documentation, you'll find an overview of MetaDefender InSights and information about each of the data feed products within the collection.

We also present information for integrators and users, describing how to [access](/docs/mdinsights/feed-access/accessing-insights-feeds) InSights threat intelligence data. This includes information about the [data format](/docs/mdinsights/data-formats/insights-data-formats) in the available feeds.
