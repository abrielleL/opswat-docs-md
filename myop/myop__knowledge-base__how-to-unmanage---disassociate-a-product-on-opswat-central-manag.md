# How to unmanage / disassociate a product on OPSWAT Central Management?

### [Unmanaging a Product in OPSWAT Central Management](/docs/myop/knowledge-base/how-to-unmanage---disassociate-a-product-on-opswat-central-manag#unmanaging-a-product-in-opswat-central-management)

To disassociate (unmanage) a specific product from the OPSWAT Central Management (OCM) console, please follow the instruction on this support article: [Deleting an existing MetaDefender Core instance](https://www.opswat.com/docs/ocm/integrations/deleting-an-existing-metadefender-core-instance).

### [On the Product Console UI](/docs/myop/knowledge-base/how-to-unmanage---disassociate-a-product-on-opswat-central-manag#on-the-product-console-ui)

To disassociate a product instance from OPSWAT Central Management using the product's console interface, follow the steps below. Ensure you are logged in as an administrator before proceeding.

1. On the top-right corner of the console, click **Managed** if the instance is currently managed by OPSWAT Central Management.
2. Click the **Disconnect** button to disassociate the product from OPSWAT Central Management.

3. Once the product is no longer managed, the label on the top-right will change to **Unmanage**, confirming that the instance has been successfully disconnected.
