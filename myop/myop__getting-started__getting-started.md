# Getting Started

To get started with My OPSWAT On-premises, you can follow the below steps:

1. Check the [System Requirements](/docs/myop/deployment/system-requirements) for My OPSWAT On-premises.
2. [Deploy](/docs/myop/deployment/deployment) My OPSWAT On-premises.
3. Configure your server.
4. [Enroll](https://docs.opswat.com/product-management/configuration/enroll-instances) your OPSWAT product instances to start managing and monitoring them.
