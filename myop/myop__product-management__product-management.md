# Central Management

Central Management is a component of My OPSWAT that allows customers to manage multiple OPSWAT products remotely, improve data flows visibility, and provide better reports for each product and entire account. It also provides critical information like product instance health, license, and connection status, report history, license, and audit trails.

More details can be found [here](https://docs.opswat.com/product-management)
