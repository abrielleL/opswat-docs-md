# User Profile

The **User Profile** page displays the information of the currently logged-in user. To open this page, open the drop-down menu next your user's full name in the top-right corner of the web page and select **User Profile**.

In this page, you can also set up or modify your user's **PIN**, which is required if the [User Setting](https://docs.opswat.com/product-management/configuration/user-settings) **Require PIN for change activities** is enabled.

To set up a new PIN

1. In the **PIN** field, Click **Set Up**
2. In the **Set Up PIN** dialog, enter and confirm your PIN.

3. Click **Save** to complete the process.

To modify your existing PIN

1. In the **PIN** field, Click **Update**
2. In the **Update PIN** dialog, enter your current PIN and the new PIN.

3. Click **Save** to complete the process.

If you have forgotten your user's PIN, please contact your administrator to reset it. Please refer to [Users & Groups](/docs/myop/user-management/user-management#users--groups) for more details.
