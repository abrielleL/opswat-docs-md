- High availability configurations for ensuring continuous operation. [Replication & HA on K8S](/docs/mdicap/v5.14.1/installation/replication---ha-on-k8s)

By following these guides, you can effectively deploy and manage MetaDefender ICAP Server in containerized environments, enhancing your security posture and ensuring the protection of your critical data.
