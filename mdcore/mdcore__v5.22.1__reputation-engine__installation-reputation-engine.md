**Reputation engine is officially supported starting from Core 5.9.0.**

Pro Tip

The Reputation Engine supports engine updates in either online or offline environments

## [Engine updates](/docs/mdcore/v5.22.1/reputation-engine/installation-reputation-engine#engine-updates)

Go to **Settings > Module Update**

MetaDefender Core will automatically download all the Engines available in your license from our online update infrastructure, and download new engine versions automatically based on your **Module Update** configuration.
