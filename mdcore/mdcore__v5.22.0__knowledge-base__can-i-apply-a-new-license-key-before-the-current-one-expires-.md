# Can I apply a new license key before the current one expires?

This article applies to all MetaDefender Core releases deployed on Windows and Linux systems.

Yes, you can apply a new license key in advance as part of your license renewal process. OPSWAT provides a grace period prior to the official start date of your renewed license to ensure a smooth transition and avoid last-minute license renewal updates.

To confirm the start date of your new key and ensure a seamless renewal, feel free to contact OPSWAT Technical Support within the week of your current license’s end date. You may also reach out to your Account Owner for further assistance.
