# Installation

**Threat Intelligence engine is officially supported starting from Core 5.17.1**

Pro-Tip

Threat Intelligence Engine supports engine updates in either offline or online enviroments

## [Engine updates](/docs/mdcore/threat-intelligence-engine/installation#engine-updates)

Go to **Settings > Module Update**

MetaDefender Core will automatically download all the Engines available in your license from our online update infrastructure, and download new engine versions automatically based on your **Module Update** configuration.
