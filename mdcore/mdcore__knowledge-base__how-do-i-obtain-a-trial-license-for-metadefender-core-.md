# How do I obtain a Trial License for MetaDefender Core?

If you’re interested in obtaining a trial license for any of our products, including MetaDefender Core, please follow the steps below:

1. Navigate to the OPSWAT **Contact** page via [This Link](https://www.opswat.com/contact)
2. Fill out the form with all of the required information.
3. Include additional details, as well as your reason for obtaining the trial license, in the **Comments** field.
4. Upon completion, click **Submit,** as highlighted in the screenshot below.
5. You should be contacted by the OPSWAT CX team shortly with a trial key for the requested product.

Support:

Should you receive no contact from OPSWAT within 48 hours of submitting the contact form, please follow the instructions below to request your trial via a support ticket:

1. Click [This Link](https://www.opswat.com/support) to visit our **Support** page.
2. Click the **Open a Case** option, as highlighted in the first screenshot below.
3. When redirected to the OPSWAT Portal, register your free OPSWAT account to open a case.
4. Log into the OPSWAT Portal.
5. Click **New**, as highlighted in the second screenshot below.
6. Choose your product and fill out all the necessary fields included in the case form.
7. Include your original details and comments, for attention: the OPSWAT CX team.

Finally, click **Save** on your support ticket and await your reply from OPSWAT.
