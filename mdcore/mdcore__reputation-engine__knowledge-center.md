# Knowledge Center

You will find here answers for FAQs about Reputation Engine.

#### [What is the minimum version of MetaDefender Core required for compatibility with the engine?](/docs/mdcore/reputation-engine/knowledge-center#what-is-the-minimum-version-of-metadefender-core-required-for-compatibility-with-the-engine)

MetaDefender core version: **5.9.0**

---

#### [How will the false detection submission impact the AV vendor's verdict?](/docs/mdcore/reputation-engine/knowledge-center#how-will-the-false-detection-submission-impact-the-av-vendors-verdict)

AV vendors' scan results are immutable; however, if the sample hash is incorporated into the Reputation Engine's database, the AV scan will be bypassed entirely.
