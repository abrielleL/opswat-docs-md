# How to enable the Removable Media Protection feature

> **Setup multi-scanning servers**
>
> You need to setup at least one multi-scanning server before enabling the feature. Following this to setup: [https://docs.opswat.com/mdendpoint/endpoint-management/setup-the-multi-scanning-server](https://docs.opswat.com/mdendpoint/endpoint-management/setup-the-multi-scanning-server)

## Enable the Removable Media Protection feature on the MetaDefender IT-OT Access

- Go to Policy Management -> Policies -> Click a policy which you want to enable the feature (Choose the Default policy on this example). As illustrated in the screenshot below.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/gukfcgwb3j8kgpjc1dx9g1ych4ms3n6vmvlbcub205o53osxk0cg0fr85l1mdi4v.png)

- Click Advanced Endpoint Protection tab and enable Threat Detection for Windows. As illustrated in the screenshot below.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/njnd1jmyv7yak19altmpnwxlps6zygdp5d8xvhto5ngq6geodubennwb8v336ao4.png)

- Enable Removable Media Protection as illustrated in the screenshot below.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/zg217zfnzqym3b4s46741teuvb38ec0vop1hcajxpvfn4cnfeckayzs4hievu116.png)

## Removable Media Protection feature on MetaDefender Endpoint

**The guide to Unblock USB**

- **Step 1:** Select USB, click on the “Unblock“ tab and then click ”Scan” to scan this USB.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/ywybfcpuwxv5h0syq0z9wjgho3ojz93ahjwdwz8vps618jxndpm193pyxy871eet.png)

- **Step 2:** Wait for the scanning process to complete and check the scan result.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/r1mmtrffbuxfyg9huiv1dn0kpsmeh1gen60j0fp5wq53ezn6hvppuqb2db8ks8wz.png)

- **Unblock Fail:** The result of unblocking USB

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/74z4ds6dgi02b2p6v4izj3na96im06p1529jgevjlnigpugmqmuazua38j5bpe38.png)

- **Unblock Success:** The result of unblocking USB

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/86fao49ywwr0rhf7egkw6yhx158xwbdzla2987c2i7lmj6w6hdz70b4pimx9vhq7.png)

**The guide to Copy file from USB**

- **Step 1**: Click on the “Copy from” tab to copy files from Media to the local drive.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/z0hqwx4d6z4jt2jn0ehxxijomzaytm90in8aq08tzpszsl71x6fz3tgmddxkp4tb.png)

- **Step 2:** Expand the media file to select the files you want to copy, then click “Next“.

**The guide to Copy file to USB**

- **Step 1**: To copy files from the local drive to Removable Media, Click on the “Copy to” tab to copy the files from the local drive to Removable Media. Click “Next” to continue.
