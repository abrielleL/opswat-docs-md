# How do I register a mobile device to a My OPSWAT Central Management account?

> **Check You Product:**
>
> This article applies to all OPSWAT Mobile App releases deployed on Android and iOS devices.

To register/enroll your mobile devices, you will need either a **Registration Code** or a **QR Code**, both of which are obtainable via My OPSWAT Central Management or via your administrator.

> **Note**
>
> Should you wish to open your own free My OPSWAT Central Management account, please [Sign Up Here](https://id.opswat.com/register), then follow the relevant instructions below.
>
> Should you wish to enroll via your organization’s account, you will need to request the registration code or QR code from your administrator, before following the relevant instructions below.

## To register via your own My OPSWAT Central Management account

- Log into [My OPSWAT Central Management](https://my.us.opswat.com/signin) and navigate to **Enroll Product** button (to enroll a device without a group) OR **Inventory>Groups>Relevant Group** (to enroll a device into a group).
- Click the **+Enroll Product** button in the top left-hand corner of the screen, as illustrated below

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/3djfr1v3roofpwx9jlq22xvemu8e7ttwffwnrvzorm643sdz970if3valnke6tko.png)

- Click **MetaDefender Endpoint > Download**. You can choose to enroll the device into a specific group at this stage

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/dzm1tiwc67lktu4gaduoj7p4xb257m5d0ihggw6gylg1l5zqut3hznfuj55vnnvw.png)

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/vdvvzlox8suq1afzgox30vqowsqp4vufz9h946mok6fcepas0y8nv2ni2yyfu8cd.png)

- On the download page, click the **Mobile & ChromeOS** tab.
- Here you will find both the **Registration Code**, the **Group Identity** (if applicable), and the **QR Code**, and you can use whichever option you prefer, as illustrated below.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/55nah220o74jdfg6p6th3lmppt6nwdrzibqn2ytfkx8alhuckkk06uwgw7vp7zjf.jpg)

- Download the free **OPSWAT Mobile App** directly from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.opswat.gears&hl=en&gl=US) or the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/opswat-mobile-app/id1037540857).
- Once installed, open the **OPSWAT Mobile App**.
- Click the **Register Your Device** button in the bottom right-hand corner of your screen, as illustrated below

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/3vtbn07oime8cuch0ooqehlrvzisjhlz6btaqbo3hsjac93m85x0e0w67scbhvqj.png)

- Next, follow the relevant instructions below, depending on whether you intend to register manually or automatically via QR Code.

## Manual Registration (Registration and Group Identity Codes)

> **Pro Tips**
>
> **Manual Registration** will require users to enter both the registration code and then the group identity code (if applicable), while the QR code is an **Automatic Registration** method that, once scanned, incorporates registration, group identity and server, without further action required from the user.

1. Enter your **Registration Code** in the field provided.
2. To add group or server details, click **Show Advanced**, then enter your **Group Identity** and select your **Server** (where applicable).
3. Click **Register Your Device** to apply the changes.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/gt7q83t3iltbjxk4uw8i9ohkezwfgs9qz5rnakvyerf0o11t465xn0uvjbyhwpid.png)

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/zj1g32ipx8vcntco5u2lhgk6u2uc9lfat74tofkb2ces31jqg5ta9cnxzbasddcx.png)

## Automatic Registration (QR Code)

1. Click the option to **Scan QR Code**.
2. Grant the OPSWAT Mobile App permission to use the device’s camera for the scan.
3. Scan the **QR Code**, and this will automatically register your device, and assign it to your chosen group (where applicable).
