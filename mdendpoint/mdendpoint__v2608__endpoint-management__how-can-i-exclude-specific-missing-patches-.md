# How can I exclude specific missing patches?

> **Check Your Product:**
>
> This article applies to the current My OPSWAT Central Management console and all MetaDefender Endpoint releases deployed on Windows systems.

My OPSWAT Central Management - Endpoint Security policies allow administrators to exclude specific missing patches from the **Patch Management** issue reporting component.

To utilize this feature and exclude specific missing patches, please follow the instructions below.

1. Log into the **My OPSWAT Central Management console** and navigate to **Endpoint Security > Policies > *****selected policy *****> Patch Management > OS Updates.**
2. Check the option to **Prevent the following OS patches from being updated**, as illustrated below:

   ![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/z4bi5jxh7r9mg5igrgld8qquzspp6r0dgjan5ta7d9albw5gj1xrkxt5ezuccgz5.png)

3. In the provided field, type out the ID of each patch to be excluded, hitting **Enter** after each name:

   ![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/bacag595shs78mnhaur4px4gq9keky2x8157jdiokl5ybo02qjq5qw9dm92e1zx3.png)

4. To remove a patch exclusion, simply click the **X** alongside the relevant patch.
5. Once all the required patches have been submitted, click **Save** to apply your settings, before confirming with your **PIN**, as required.
