# How to export the MetaDefender Endpoint logs from my mobile device?

> **Check Your Version:**
>
> This article applies to all MetaDefender Endpoint releases deployed on iOS or Android devices.

#### If you are experiencing technical issues with the **OPSWAT MetaDefender Endpoint** mobile app, our support team may request your logs to diagnose the problem.

#### Follow these steps to generate and export a bug report from your device.

1. Open the MetaDefender mobile app on your device and navigate to the **Settings** page (represented by a gear icon).

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/dv8dgfy4k821iozbg8zybs5bitudj92qj3rnl5diitfzozvnjydc1xr1yddr9rtf.png)

2. Locate and tap the Report a Bug button. This action automatically bundles the necessary diagnostic logs.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/pmpz40e9uixozm1dxzw9mf1ch7yi7zrzfpz1x6bqk46zhpy97mzibctr1k1dra6i.png)

3.Enter a brief description of the problem you are encountering. Once finished, click **Send**.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/gjh9t3kym335w1l4gr3vzmf840ltg0xgdl3zw7hf4k9sdykm9cnbmja94nhnqwc2.png)

4. A system menu will appear asking how you want to share the file. Select your preferred application, such as:
   - **Email:** Outlook, Gmail, or Mail.
   - **Messaging:** Teams, WhatsApp, or Slack.

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/nh0bobhezy74x7iqjazontoxxr7fywe0c0mqm6ib5094o4mj45sxh030e5iy1hof.png)

5. If you selected an email client, enter the recipient's address. You can send it directly to an OPSWAT representative or to your own inbox.

Android:

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/6r1xptjsu9i0zg7b0kvfpdj69f1dln2tjcf3hlb1qgw45kbwgkm9hn3d0z927rka.png)

SApple iOA:

![](https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/2x2P/9nrai2cyonm66ch8r3eg1s95p225gak2z4x58j3ddvhffyfruiolzjt8lwry72dc.png)

6. If you sent the logs to yourself:
   - Open the email on your computer or mobile device.
   - Save the ZIP attachment
   - **Upload the file** directly to your active OPSWAT support ticket.

> **Support:**
>
> If **Further Assistance** is required, please proceed to log a [**support case or chat with one of our support engineers**](https://my.opswat.com/support).
