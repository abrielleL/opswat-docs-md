# Scanning with embedded anti-malware engines

## What's this feature about?

The introduction of embedded anti-malware engines scanning in MetaDefender Endpoint represents a significant advancement in endpoint security, addressing critical customer needs such as continuous protection, improved scanning performance, and network independence. Whether working remotely, in air-gapped environments, or handling sensitive data, our customers now benefit from enhanced operational efficiency and robust protection against removable media threats.

The embedded anti-malware engines in MetaDefender Endpoint ensures files from removable media are scanned under all conditions. When internet connectivity is available, the files can be scanned using our powerful MetaDefender Core or MetaDefender Cloud, delivering advanced threat detection capabilities. When connectivity is interrupted or unavailable, the embedded AV automatically takes over, ensuring continuous protection without disruption.
