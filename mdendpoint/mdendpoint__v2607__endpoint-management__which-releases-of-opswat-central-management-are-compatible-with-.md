# Which releases of OPSWAT Central Management are compatible with MetaDefender Endpoint?

> **Check Your Product:**
>
> This article applies to OPSWAT Central Management V7.0+ and a wide range of MetaDefender Endpoint releases deployed on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and Android systems.

> **Please Note:**
>
> OPSWAT Central Management V7+ does not check the validity of the versions of the MetaDefender Endpoint install package that may be manually uploaded to a server for prospective endpoint users to download.
>
> Instead, if an MetaDefender Endpoint version is incompatible with OPSWAT Central Management  V7+, the devices on which those MetaDefender Endpoint versions are installed will simply not be able to connect to or communicate with the OPSWAT Central Management  server.
