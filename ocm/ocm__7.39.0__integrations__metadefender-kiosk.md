# MetaDefender Kiosk Windows

MetaDefender Kiosk Windows helps protect your network by enabling control over the flow of data into and out of your organization. It can be used as a media scanning station on your own hardware or on OPSWAT's custom-made kiosks. Media such as USB devices, DVDs, SD cards, flash drives, or floppy disks are processed by MetaDefender Kiosk Windows by inserting the media device into the appropriate drive.
