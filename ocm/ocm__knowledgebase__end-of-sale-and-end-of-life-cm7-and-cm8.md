# End of Sale and End of Life for CM7 and CM8

OPSWAT announces End-of-Sale and End-of-Life for OPSWAT Central Management version 7 and My OPSWAT On-premises (Central Management version 8). The last day to order the affected products is July 31, 2025. Customers with active service contracts will continue to receive service and support under the terms and conditions of customers' service contract until the expiration date of their contract or the affected product's end of life. Table 1 describes the end-of-sale milestones, definitions, and dates for the affected service

During support cycles, OPSWAT will provide fixes for reproducible and verifiable defects in End-of-Sale products when there is no workaround and the defect is preventing the use of the products. Fixes will be provided in the next release of products after confirmation of the defect.

Table 1. End-of-Sale Milestones and Dates for the OPSWAT Central Management version 7 and My OPSWAT On-premises (Central Management version 8)

| Milestone | Definition | Date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| End-of-Sale Announcement Date | The date the document that announces the end-of-sale and end-of-life of a product is distributed to the general public. | 05/01/2025 |
| End-of-Sale Date | The last date to order the product through OPSWAT point-of-sale mechanisms. The product is no longer for sale after this date. | 07/31/2025 |
| End of Maintenance Releases Date | The last date that OPSWAT may release any final software maintenance releases or bug fixes. After this date, OPSWAT will no longer develop, repair, maintain, or test the product software. | 01/31/2027 |
| End-of-Life Date | The last date to receive applicable service and support for the product as entitled by active support service contracts or by warranty terms and conditions. After this date, all support services for the product are unavailable, and the product becomes obsolete. | 01/31/2027 |

## [Product Migration Options](/docs/ocm/knowledgebase/end-of-sale-and-end-of-life-cm7-and-cm8#product-migration-options)

Customers are encouraged to migrate to [My OPSWAT Central Management version 10](https://www.opswat.com/products/metadefender/my-opswat-central-management).

My OPSWAT Central Management version 10 is an all-in-one platform for managing and monitoring OPSWAT products and solutions. From a single pane-of-glass, remotely manage instances, perform global operations, and handle updates and licensing. Available in the cloud and on-premises, My OPSWAT Central Management simplifies central management while enhancing visibility and control.

This [page](https://www.opswat.com/docs/cm/product-management) shows what products My OPSWAT Central Management version 10 supports and support level.
