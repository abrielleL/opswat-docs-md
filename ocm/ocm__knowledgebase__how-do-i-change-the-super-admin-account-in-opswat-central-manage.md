# How do I change the Super Admin Account in OPSWAT Central Management v7?

**Check Your Version:** This article applies to OPSWAT Central Management versions after 7.37 deployed on Windows.

## [**Steps to Configure**](/docs/ocm/knowledgebase/how-do-i-change-the-super-admin-account-in-opswat-central-manage#steps-to-configure)

To change the Super Admin account, please contact the OPSWAT support team for assistance.

1. Provide the support team with the **Deployment ID** of your OCM instance.
2. The support team will provide you with a tool and the steps to change the Super Admin account.

**Support:** If **Further Assistance** is required, please proceed to log a [support case or chatting with our support engineer](https://my.opswat.com/support).
